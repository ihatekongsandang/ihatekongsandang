This post is based on the original caption and the first image. The original is a post from Rep. Kim Tae-gyu (김태규)'s own account, and it says the full commentary is on the People Power Party website. Another post on the same issue (may be available only in Korean): [Summary of the dispute over a new justice recommendation](/post/2026-09-18-dailybite-judicial-independence).

## What reports confirm

- On Sept. 22, Chief Justice Cho Hee-dae (조희대) reportedly refused President Lee Jae-myung's request to submit a new Supreme Court justice recommendation, saying it had no constitutional basis, and the People Power Party reportedly called it "an important decision to protect the independence of the judiciary."
- Rep. Kim Tae-gyu reportedly said, "The presidential office's attempt to neutralize the power of recommendation — the chief justice's inherent authority — by invoking a vague custom goes beyond the rule of law and is an act contrary to the Constitution," adding, "Upholding the principle this time was the right thing to do." He reportedly explained that under the Constitution, authority over appointing Supreme Court justices is divided among the chief justice's recommendation, the National Assembly's consent and the president's appointment.
- Earlier, on Sept. 18, President Lee reportedly said, "I think we need to consider why independence is granted to state institutions."

## What we left out of the summary

The assessment that the request for a new recommendation is unconstitutional or an abuse of authority is a claim by Rep. Kim and others in the opposition; no legal ruling has been made on it.
