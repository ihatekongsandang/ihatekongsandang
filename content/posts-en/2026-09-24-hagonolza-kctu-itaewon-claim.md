This post is based on the original caption and the first image. The caption cites an MBN report as its source. Another post on the same case: [Itaewon directive case — sentence reduced on appeal and finalized by the Supreme Court](/post/2026-07-19-amhaengeosa-kctu-itaewon-directive).

## What reports confirm

- Former Korean Confederation of Trade Unions (KCTU) organizing and labor-dispute bureau chief Mr. Seok was reportedly indicted in May 2023 on charges including espionage and reported to have received North Korean directives 102 times between 2018 and 2022.
- The directive concerning the Itaewon disaster was reportedly an email received **around Nov. 15, 2022, about two weeks after the disaster**, instructing them, "taking this extraordinary disaster as an opportunity … actively carry out organizing work to unleash, to the fullest, the anger of all walks of life."
- Reports say the first-instance court (Suwon District Court, November 2024) sentenced Seok to 15 years in prison, but the appellate court (May 2025) found him not guilty on some charges — for example, not recognizing the existence of a secret organization — and reduced the sentence to nine years and six months, and that the Supreme Court finalized it on Sept. 25, 2025.

## What we left out of the summary

The attached image's "North Korean directive to cause the Itaewon disaster" and "it happened because a union official said OK" **differ from what was reported.** The reported directive was to exploit the disaster after it had happened, and investigations and rulings did not find that anyone caused the disaster. The "15 years in prison" in the caption is the first-instance sentence.
