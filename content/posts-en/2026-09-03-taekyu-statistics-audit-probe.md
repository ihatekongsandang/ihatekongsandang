This post is based on the original caption and the first image. The original is a post from Rep. Kim Tae-gyu (김태규)'s own account, and it says the full commentary is on the People Power Party website.

## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- It is not the people who should be held responsible for manipulating statistics but the officials who uncovered it who are being investigated, and it asks whether this is the "truth-finding" the Democratic Party talks about.

## What reports confirm

- On Aug. 27, 2026, the Board of Audit and Inspection (BAI) reportedly announced that its inspection of the "real estate statistics audit" and the "audit of the killing of a public official in the West Sea" of the Moon Jae-in administration, both conducted under the Yoon Suk Yeol administration, found repeated high-handed conduct, investigations tailored to fit conclusions, false recording of answers and illegal forensics.
- In connection with the statistics audit, the BAI reportedly filed criminal complaints against nine members of the audit team on charges including abuse of authority, requested heavy disciplinary action against 10 (including six dismissals and one removal), and handed over investigation reference materials on 10 people, including former BAI chairman Choe Jae-hae (최재해) and audit commissioner Yoo Byung-ho (유병호), to the National Office of Investigation. Twelve people involved in the West Sea audit reportedly received written warnings because the statute of limitations for disciplinary action had passed.
- The People Power Party reportedly criticized that the officials who raised and investigated suspicions of statistics manipulation had instead become targets of investigation.

## What we left out of the summary

The assessment that "those who manipulated are fine" is the opposition's claim. We could not confirm from the reports we found this time the status of the trial in the case indicted over suspicions of statistics manipulation under the Moon Jae-in administration.
