## What reports confirm

People Power Party supreme council member Kim Min-soo (김민수) reportedly made the following remarks at a party meeting on July 20.

- He said "defense is not something to reform but something to strengthen," criticizing the government for pushing to earmark a budget before announcing its defense reform blueprint in October as getting the order backward (OhmyNews).
- He said that if the commander-in-chief lacks understanding of the military, the defense minister must have expertise, but that currently both lack an understanding of the military (Gukje News (국제뉴스)).
- He expressed a critical view of remarks linking the military academy merger to preventing coups (The Public (더퍼블릭)).

## Scope of verification

Statements in the caption such as "strengthening joint Army-Navy-Air Force operational capabilities and Korea–U.S. combined exercises is the right direction" and "the views of former officers in the party should be reflected" could not be checked against individual reports this time. References to military service were reported as supreme council member Kim's remarks, and this post does not evaluate them.
