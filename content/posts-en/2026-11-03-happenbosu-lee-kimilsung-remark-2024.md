This post is based on the original caption and thumbnail (one video frame, a KBS News screen); the full video was not reviewed. The original was posted on Nov. 3, 2026, but the remark it covers was made on **Jan. 19, 2024**, when Lee was leader of the Democratic Party. The thumbnail line "I doubted my own eyes — people, please wake up" is the poster's opinion, so we used a card made by this site.

## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- Billed as a "resurfaced past remark," it says then-Democratic Party leader Lee Jae-myung, while urging North Korea to stop military provocations, said Kim Jong Un should make efforts so that the efforts of Kim Il Sung and Kim Jong Il are not belittled.
- It says the People Power Party criticized this as a security gaffe showing an inappropriate view of North Korea.

## What reports confirm

- At a Supreme Council meeting on Jan. 19, 2024, then party leader Lee Jae-myung (이재명) said Kim Jong Un must stop missile provocations, adding that Kim Jong Un "should make efforts so that the efforts of the predecessors, our North Korea's Kim Jong Il and President Kim Il Sung, are not belittled or damaged," according to reports (New Daily).
- People Power Party spokesperson Kim Ye-ryeong (김예령) said the same day, "What did Kim Il Sung, who started the Korean War, and Kim Jong Il, who laid the groundwork for the nuclear threat, contribute to peace on the Korean Peninsula?", and members of the National Assembly defense and foreign affairs committees demanded a retraction and a public apology, it was reported (Newsis).
