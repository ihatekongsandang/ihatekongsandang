This post is based on the original text and its three images (captures of a May 22, 2024 New Daily article), and we used a card made by this site instead of the original images. The author says they are the lawyer who filed the suit, but this post did not separately verify the account holder's identity. We do not reproduce derogatory wording such as "Red-unification ministry."

## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- It says the author filed an information disclosure suit over former President Moon Jae-in's "Panmunjom USB," lost and paid 13.5 million won in litigation costs to the unification ministry, but obtained a ruling that the USB contained Grade 3 secrets. It says Moon is now under investigation after an espionage complaint, "until (Moon) goes to prison."
- The captured New Daily headline reads "The 'Panmunjom USB' Moon handed to Kim Jong-un… was a 'Grade 3 state secret.'"

## What reports confirm

- On May 17, 2024, the Seoul Administrative Court's 3rd Administrative Division ruled against lawyer Ku Ju-wa (구주와), spokesperson for the Liberty Unification Party, in a suit against the unification minister seeking to overturn a refusal to disclose information, it was reported. The court reportedly found that the USB held by the ministry contained North Korea policy and inter-Korean cooperation material on the "New Economic Initiative for the Korean Peninsula" prepared ahead of the 2018 Panmunjom summit, that the information was designated and managed as a Grade 3 secret under the National Intelligence Service Act and security regulations, and that disclosure could seriously harm national interests.
- On April 23, 2025, the Seoul Central District Prosecutors' Office dismissed complaints accusing Moon and others of espionage, benefiting the enemy and other offenses, reportedly stating that handing over the USB was "part of governing activity" in pursuing the New Economic Initiative on inter-Korean economic cooperation and could not be seen as providing military benefit to the North.
- Ku's damages suit against Moon over the same act was dismissed at first instance — "the evidence submitted does not establish that the defendant committed espionage, benefited the enemy or committed treason" — and on appeal in June 2026, according to reports. The unification ministry has reportedly said the USB material did not even contain the word "nuclear power plant."
- New complaints accusing Moon of espionage and other charges were reportedly filed with police in January 2026 by another group of lawyers and others, and in March by YouTuber Jeon Han-gil (전한길).

## What we left out of the summary

- The 2024 ruling found that the contents of the USB held by the unification ministry are designated as Grade 3 secrets and cannot be disclosed; it did not determine whether it is the same USB actually given to Kim Jong-un or whether Moon's act amounted to espionage or benefiting the enemy. The captured headline "handed to Kim Jong-un… was a Grade 3 state secret" should be read in that context.
- We could not confirm the original's "13.5 million won in litigation costs" or the complainant and status of the investigation it says is under way.
