This post is based on the original caption and the first image. The Twitter sentences transcribed in the image were not reproduced in this post.

## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- It has already been reported that "Hyegyeonggung Kim" (혜경궁 김씨) is first lady Kim Hye-kyung (김혜경), and the posts in the image are comments she left around the time President Lee Jae-myung entered politics.

## What reports confirm

- In 2018, the Gyeonggi Southern Provincial Police Agency reportedly analyzed some 40,000 posts from the "Hyegyeonggung Kim" Twitter account (@08__hkkim), along with mobile phone subscription information and other data, concluded that the account user and Kim Hye-kyung were the same person, and referred the case to prosecutors with a recommendation to indict.
- On Dec. 11, 2018, prosecutors reportedly decided not to indict, saying "there is insufficient evidence to conclude that Kim Hye-kyung alone owned or used the account," and citing as reasons that the screen purporting to show the account's registered email was a screenshot circulated online and could not serve as evidence, and that the email account had been shared by several people, so the author could not be identified.
- The police reportedly issued an unusual statement calling the prosecutors' decision "somewhat unexpected."

## What we left out of the summary

That "Hyegyeonggung Kim is Kim Hye-kyung" was the police's conclusion at the referral stage; prosecutors declined to indict for insufficient evidence, so it has not been legally established. We also did not separately check this time whether the posts in the image were actually posted on that account.
