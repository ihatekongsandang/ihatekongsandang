This post is based on the original text and one attached image. Profanity in the original text was not reproduced.

## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- It says Rep. Jeon Hyun-hee (전현희) proposed "a law under which only the state owns land and individuals can own only buildings," and that this is dragging the country toward communism.

## What reports confirm

- Democratic Party Rep. Jeon Hyun-hee reportedly sponsored, on Sept. 21, 2026, a partial amendment to the Housing and Urban Fund Act that would allow the Housing and Urban Fund to support the costs of building land-lease sale housing and preparing its sites.
- In land-lease sale housing, a public entity such as LH keeps the land and sells only the buildings, lowering buyers' land costs; Rep. Jeon reportedly claimed that this method "would make it possible to sell even a 30-pyeong-class apartment in Gangnam for around 300 million won."

## What we left out of the summary

As reported, the bill provides fund support for publicly owned land only in the case of housing supplied under the land-lease method; **reports do not confirm any provision making all land owned only by the state or banning individuals from owning land.** The text in the original image differs from the content of the bill.
