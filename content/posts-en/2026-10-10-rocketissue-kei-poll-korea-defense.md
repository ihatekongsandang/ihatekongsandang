This post is based on the original caption, and we used a card made by this site instead of the original image. The original caption does not name the polling organization, so we add the name confirmed in reports.

## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- It says 71% of Americans support sending U.S. forces to defend South Korea if North Korea invades, with 77% of Republicans and 73% of Democrats in favor; that 58% support South Korea developing nuclear-powered submarines and 71% oppose it developing nuclear weapons; that 64% say the alliance helps U.S. national security; and that 55% support a Trump–Kim Jong-un summit.

## What reports confirm

- The Korea Economic Institute of America (KEI), a U.S. policy institute, released results on Oct. 8 (local time) of a poll of Americans on the alliance conducted with YouGov, finding that 71% support using U.S. forces to defend South Korea if the North invades, it was reported. KEI has run the survey annually since 2020.
- 58% supported South Korea developing nuclear-powered submarines (56% of Democrats, 64% of Republicans), and 71% opposed South Korea developing nuclear weapons, according to reports.
- 68% of Americans viewed South Korea favorably, 64% said the alliance serves U.S. national security interests and 68% viewed North Korea negatively, it was reported. 56% favored maintaining or increasing U.S. forces in Korea, far more than those favoring reductions (12%) or withdrawal (6%).
