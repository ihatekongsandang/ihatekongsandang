This post is based on the original caption; the video content was not reviewed. Another post of the same kind (may be available only in Korean): [Sept. 12 march in downtown Gwangju](/post/2026-09-13-geonwoo-gwangju-rally).

## What the original post reports (the poster's account)

- On a weekend before Sept. 19, 2026, citizens holding Taegeukgi flags and pickets marched to the beat of drums through the streets of Hongdae in Seoul.
- Participants chanted slogans such as "The country is a mess — stop going on overseas trips" and "Gyeonggi Province has declared bankruptcy — stop splashing taxpayers' money around."
- The poster claimed that such voices from the field are not sufficiently covered by the media and asked people to share the post.

## What we left out of the summary

The chants relayed by the original, "denounce the computer-rigged presidential election and elections" and "same-day voting, hand counting," presuppose election fraud; no National Election Commission or court finding, or press report, supporting this was confirmed, so they were not included in the summary. We could not confirm the date of the march, the number of participants or the organizing group.
