## What reports confirm

- Around 10:50 a.m. on Sept. 21, two blasts presumed to be mines reportedly occurred during an Army 25th Division DMZ search operation, injuring three officers and NCOs, including a combat engineer staff sergeant in the lead and the search battalion commander (a lieutenant colonel), who was moving to take safety measures. One of the three reportedly had a foot amputated at the ankle.
- The operation reportedly deployed 18 personnel from the 25th Division, including 15 officers and NCOs and 3 enlisted soldiers.

## What we left out of the summary

The claims that "enlisted soldiers were also injured" and that "casualties are close to 10" originated on online communities, and the original post itself states these have not been confirmed by an official military announcement. We could not find reports supporting these claims.
