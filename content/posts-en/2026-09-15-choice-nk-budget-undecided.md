## What reports confirm

- According to material People Power Party Rep. Ahn Cheol-soo (안철수) obtained from the Ministry of Unification, the 2026 budgets earmarked for relief aid and livelihood cooperation are reportedly 112.23 billion won and 555.38 billion won, respectively, for a total of 667.61 billion won.
- Of this, the spending plans for three programs — relief aid (112.23 billion won), support for North Korean infants and others (155.815 billion won) and cooperation in agriculture, livestock, forestry and the environment (247.736 billion won), totaling 515.781 billion won (77.3%) — were reportedly listed as "undecided" as of September.
- Government humanitarian aid actually delivered to North Korean residents last year reportedly amounted to zero won.
- The ministry reportedly explained that it increased the earmarked budget for humanitarian cooperation projects, such as support for North Korean infants and health and medical cooperation, to support its policy of peaceful coexistence on the Korean Peninsula.
