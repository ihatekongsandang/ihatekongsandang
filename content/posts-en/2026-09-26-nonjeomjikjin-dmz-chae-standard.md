This post is based on the original caption; the card image content was not reviewed.

## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- In the 2023 case of Cpl. Chae Su-geun (채수근), then-Democratic Party leader Lee Jae-myung defined the death in the line of duty as man-made and, saying the government's own investigation was insufficient, demanded truth-finding through a special counsel.
- Even now, under a different administration, the DMZ blast should be clarified by the same standard: why the troops were injured, whether there was a problem in the operational judgment, and what the explosive was.

## What reports confirm

- Reports say that around 10:50 a.m. on Sept. 21, 2026, a combat engineer staff sergeant clearing the search route in the lead was injured first in a blast during a DMZ search operation, and that the search battalion commander (a lieutenant colonel), who was moving to take safety measures, was caught in a second blast. Three officers were injured, one of whom reportedly had a foot amputated at the ankle.
- Whether the explosive was a North Korean mine had reportedly not yet been confirmed.
- Democratic Party Rep. Park Jie-won (박지원) reportedly said on Sept. 26, "If our soldiers were hurt in a mine accident, the public will understand only if the truth is disclosed as it is," and "Why can't you just shut up and explain? Why do you hide instead of acting proactively?" — criticizing the government's response on both the DMZ accident and the repatriation of North Korean POWs.
- Then-party leader Lee Jae-myung reportedly said in August 2023 regarding the Cpl. Chae Su-geun case that "no one in the government is taking responsibility," criticizing the government for covering up the truth and demanding truth-finding through a special counsel.

## What we left out of the summary

We could not find a report using the exact phrase quoted in the original, "Cpl. Chae's death in the line of duty was thoroughly man-made." There are reports to the effect that Lee defined the case as man-made at the time, but we could not confirm his exact wording.
