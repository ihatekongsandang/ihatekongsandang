This post is based on the original text ("This is 100% clearly a case of covering for North Korea and covering up the incident") and one attached image. The image pairs a quote with a photo of what appears to be the UN Command commander, making it read like his own words, but reports present the same content as **a claim by Rep. Han Dong-hoon (한동훈)**.

## What reports confirm

- Independent lawmaker Han Dong-hoon reportedly claimed on Sept. 26 that "UNC Commander Xavier Brunson ordered a swift investigation on the day of the accident, the 21st, and on the 22nd the UNC Military Armistice Commission visited the 25th Division and requested that materials, body-cam footage and interviews with the injured be shared and that it be allowed access to the site. Our military refused this request."
- Han reportedly said, "A specific refusal of the UNC's request is not something that could happen at the 25th Division commander's level," and demanded that whoever refused be identified.
- The JCS reportedly said on the 25th that "the UNC Military Armistice Commission visited the 25th Division on the 22nd, confirmed the circumstances of the blast and related matters, and coordinated on the joint site investigation," and that "the claim that our military stalled on the UNC's request for an investigation is also not true," countering that cooperation continued with a preliminary inquiry on the 26th and the site investigation on the 27th.

## What we left out of the summary

"100% clearly covering for North Korea and covering up the incident" is the original poster's own conclusion and was not confirmed by reports. We also could not find a report that the UN Command commander himself spoke the words shown in the image.
