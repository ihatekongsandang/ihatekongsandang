## What reports confirm

- At the National Assembly's interpellation session on political affairs on Sept. 9, 2026, when People Power Party Rep. Yoon Jae-ok (윤재옥) asked, "Do you know what the Lee Jae-myung government's No. 1 national goal is?", Prime Minister Han Sung-sook (한성숙) reportedly answered, "Yes, it's a constitutional amendment. Related."
- When Rep. Yoon, surprised, asked back, "Pardon?", Prime Minister Han reportedly corrected herself: "Ah, the national agenda — politics that unites the people." It was reportedly her first appearance at an interpellation session since taking office in July.
- Asked about the president's falling approval ratings, Prime Minister Han reportedly answered, "We are taking the voices of the public seriously" and "We are painfully aware of it."
- People Power Party leader Jang Dong-hyuk (장동혁) reportedly criticized the answer as "true intentions slipping out," taking issue with what he called the president's intent to amend the Constitution to allow reelection.

## What we left out of the summary

That "the president is desperate for a constitutional amendment because of a reelection amendment" is leader Jang Dong-hyuk's claim; the government's or ruling party's intentions in pursuing a constitutional amendment have not been confirmed as fact.
