## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- West Sea shooting: It criticizes that even though it is unclear how the official died after being dragged away by North Korean troops, the Moon Jae-in (문재인) administration framed the victim as a gambling addict, concluded he had voluntarily defected, and failed to protest to North Korea.
- The "second box-mine incident": It criticizes that a soldier had his foot amputated at the ankle after a North Korean mine blast, yet the Lee Jae-myung administration could not even call it a North Korean mine and went to the UN only to defend North Korea.

## What reports confirm

- In September 2020, a Ministry of Oceans and Fisheries official was shot and killed by North Korean forces in the West Sea. The Coast Guard at the time judged it a voluntary defection, but reportedly reversed that conclusion in June 2022, saying it "found no evidence to support intent to defect."
- Former National Security Office chief Suh Hoon (서훈), former NIS director Park Jie-won (박지원), former Defense Minister Suh Wook (서욱) and former Coast Guard commissioner Kim Hong-hee (김홍희), who were indicted on charges of covering up and distorting the case, were reportedly all acquitted in the first trial on Dec. 26, 2025, for "lack of proof of the crime," and the bereaved family reportedly objected.
- President Lee reportedly laid out mutual respect between the two Koreas' systems, North Korea-US dialogue and phased denuclearization in his keynote address to the UN General Assembly on Sept. 22 (local time); we could not find a report that he directly mentioned the DMZ mine accident in the speech. The JCS announced on Sept. 28, after the speech, that "the possibility a North Korean mine caused it is very high," and President Lee said on the 29th that "we will take the necessary measures according to the truth."
