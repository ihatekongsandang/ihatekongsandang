This post is based on the original caption. The original was posted on Sept. 18, and the chief justice's decision to refuse came afterward, on Sept. 22.

## What reports confirm

- In his closing address at the 20th Conference of Chief Justices of Asia and the Pacific on Sept. 18, Chief Justice Cho Hee-dae (조희대) reportedly said, "The independence and fairness of the judiciary, its integrity and the public's trust must always be protected," emphasizing judicial independence again after his opening address the previous day.
- At a press conference the same day, President Lee Jae-myung reportedly answered a question about the dispute over a new Supreme Court justice recommendation by saying, "I think we need to consider why independence is granted to state institutions." He reportedly did not specifically address whether the request for a new recommendation was justified.
- On Sept. 22, Chief Justice Cho reportedly refused the president's request for a new Supreme Court justice recommendation, saying it had no constitutional basis, and the People Power Party reportedly called it "an important decision to protect the independence of the judiciary."
