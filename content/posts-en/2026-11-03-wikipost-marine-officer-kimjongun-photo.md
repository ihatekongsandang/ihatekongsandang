This post is based on the original caption and one image. The original image's text, "Marines warn of punishment for disparaging the North / A country that tries to punish people for criticizing North Korea," is a definitive claim that differs from the Marine Corps' explanation, and the photo shows several service members, so we used a card made by this site.

## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- It says a claim arose that a Marine officer faced possible discipline after posting on social media a photo with a slogan criticizing Kim Jong Un, and also notes that the Marine Corps said it had advised deleting the photo over military security concerns about the training ground and that the claim it sought discipline over disparaging the North was untrue.
- It then argues that military security and criticism of the North Korean regime should be kept separate, and that freedom of expression should be protected and the allegations explained transparently.

## What reports confirm

- A YouTuber who is a former Army officer claimed on social media on Nov. 1 that a Marine engineer battalion officer had been warned of possible discipline after posting a post-training photo on a personal account; the photo showed service members in uniform and combat gear holding a banner reading "Let's grind up and drink Kim Jong Un," according to reports.
- The Marine Corps reportedly said in an official statement on Nov. 2 that the claim it threatened discipline over disparaging the North was untrue. According to the Marines, the officer posted four photos, including images of the inside of a training ground, after a joint exercise, and a senior officer who saw them advised deleting them, saying the posts "could be punished as a violation of the military security regulations"; the Marines said no disciplinary process was under way over posts disparaging the North, it was reported.
- The YouTuber who first raised the claim reportedly said again that material passed on by the senior officer stated that content disparaging North Korea could be punished under the National Security Act (Dailian).
