## What reports confirm

- The People Power Party reportedly held a large rally at the National Assembly on Sept. 22, 2026, attended by leader Jang Dong-hyuk and floor leader Jung Jeom-sik (정점식) among others, with Jang reportedly repeating the chant "resume Lee Jae-myung's trial" along with attendees.
- President Lee Jae-myung's trial on election-law charges and other cases has reportedly been suspended since he took office, and People Power Party lawmakers reportedly also raised the need to resume the trial at a Sept. 14 Supreme Court justice nominee hearing.
