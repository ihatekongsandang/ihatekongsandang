## What reports confirm

- At a Cabinet meeting on Sept. 29, President Lee Jae-myung reportedly said, "Three soldiers were seriously injured in a mine accident near the Military Demarcation Line last week," offering comfort to their families and wishing them a speedy recovery.
- He reportedly said, "The military authorities must uncover the truth transparently through a swift and thorough investigation in close cooperation with the UN Command," and "we will take the necessary measures according to the truth."
- He reportedly added, "I hope there will be no endangering of national security by stoking groundless conspiracy theories."
- The presidential office reportedly said the timing of the military's site investigation had nothing to do with the president's UN speech and was decided by the Joint Chiefs of Staff and the UN Command on military grounds, taking field commanders' recommendations into account.

## What we left out of the summary

- Reports did not specify whose claims the president meant by "conspiracy theories," so we did not speculate.
