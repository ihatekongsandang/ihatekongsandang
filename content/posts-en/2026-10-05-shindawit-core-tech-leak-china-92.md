## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- The first image reads "Taking secrets through disguised employment / Economic security has a hole — 'top emergency'," and the second captures the headline and subheadings of an Oct. 5 Maeil Business Newspaper article.
- It says that of 25 national core technology overseas leak cases uncovered from 2022 to August this year, 23 (92%) went to China and 18 (72%) involved insiders such as employees; that some set up fake consulting firms to request information gradually; that a former Samsung executive set up a Chinese company and poached 200 engineers; and that CXMT even obtained Samsung's DRAM "process recipe."

## What reports confirm

- According to parliamentary audit data that People Power Party Rep. Cho Ji-yeon (조지연) obtained from the National Police Agency, 23 (92%) of the 25 national core technology overseas leak cases that police uncovered and sent to prosecutors from 2022 to August this year involved leaks to China, and 18 (72%) of the 25 were caused by insiders such as employees or researchers of the victim companies, it was reported. By year, all 4 cases in 2022 and both cases in 2023 involved China, as did 10 of 11 in 2024, 5 of 6 last year and both cases through August this year.
- The same data reportedly showed industrial technology leak cases rose 45.5% to 179 last year from 123 the year before, and overseas leak cases rose from 27 to 33.
- Reports said there were also cases of using companies disguised as consulting firms to employ researchers, or offering high advisory fees and then requesting sensitive technical information. Reports also said a former Samsung Electronics executive set up a company with the local government of Chengdu, China, and offered higher pay and other incentives, with signs that about 200 semiconductor workers moved to China; and that in the case involving Samsung Electronics' 18-nanometer DRAM process information and CXMT, investigators believe a "process recipe" covering the order, equipment, materials and detailed settings of more than 600 manufacturing steps was leaked.

## What we left out of the summary

- The first image's "disguised employment" shortens the Maeil Business headline's "getting hired at a disguised company"; the reported cases involve information requests through companies disguised as consulting firms and the like.
- The individual cases above were reported as being on trial or as investigation findings, so this post does not name individuals.
