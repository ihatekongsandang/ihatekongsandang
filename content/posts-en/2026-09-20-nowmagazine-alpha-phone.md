This post is based on the original caption. The original states on its image that it "includes content reconstructed using AI," and the image content was not reviewed.

## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- There is critical public opinion asking whether blocking access to social media, even with the aim of protecting young people, is anachronistic control.

## What reports confirm

- At a closed-door Supreme Council meeting on Sept. 18, the Democratic Party reportedly voted to form an "Alpha Phone Project TF: a student-only smartphone for Generation Alpha," chaired by Rep. Kim Young-ho (김영호).
- The Alpha Phone is reportedly a smartphone that allows functions such as calls, text messages, location checks and transit cards while restricting access to social media, games and harmful content, with the aim of reducing smartphone overdependence and exposure to harmful content.
- Rep. Kim reportedly said, "The goal is to launch the Alpha Phone after the TF wraps up its work by July or August next year," and "We plan to begin the public deliberation process in Seoul from Oct. 30."
- Rep. Kim has reportedly proposed spreading the phone through Ministry of Education certification, social consensus and cooperation with manufacturers, rather than mandating its use by law.

## What we left out of the summary

We could not confirm which survey the original's "approval among young people plunging to the 10% range" comes from. Expressions such as "Communist Party-style thinking" are online reactions relayed by the original, and reports do not confirm anything about mandating use of the Alpha Phone by law.
