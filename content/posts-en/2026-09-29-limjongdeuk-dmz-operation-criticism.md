## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- "In 38 years in the military, I have never seen an operation like this," criticizing the operation.

## What reports confirm

- At the Defense Committee on Sept. 29, Rep. Lim Jong-deuk (임종득), a retired Army major general, reportedly said the blast "was not a chance accident during a patrol, but happened while clearing a path to verify evidence of mines laid across the MDL," and, noting that a planned operation left only body-camera footage, said, "In 38 years in the military I have never seen an operation conducted that way."
- He criticized sending 18 troops into a dangerous area without adequate preparation and, asking whether the response had slackened after a directive under the previous minister not to apply the rules of engagement mechanically, demanded that the cause of the security failure be identified and those responsible punished.
- The JCS reportedly said it had never ordered any deviation from rules and procedures, and Defense Minister Kang Shin-chul (강신철) said, "I apologize for what was not complete in this process," while maintaining that response principles had not been violated.
- At the time of the accident, the troops had reportedly cleared the brush and used detectors to open the route, but the resin (plastic) mine was not detected.

## What we left out of the summary

- The "directive not to apply the rules of engagement mechanically" is an allegation raised by Lim; the JCS denied issuing any such order.
