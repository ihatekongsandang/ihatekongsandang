## What the original says (poster's introduction and video captions)

- It says People Power Party lawmaker Park Choong-kwon (박충권) staged a filibuster against a partial amendment to the National Assembly Act, addressing North Korea's nuclear and missile threats and problems with the government reorganization based on experience in ICBM development research in North Korea and expertise in science, technology and defense.
- The video carries title captions such as "Leave the Democratic Party's bad laws and we'll sink into a populist dictatorship like Venezuela," "Radio psychological warfare against the North is the most effective defense and offense," "We must not give North Korea any more time or money" and "The very presence of U.S. Forces Korea is nuclear deterrence."

## What reports confirm

- Rep. Park held a filibuster at the plenary session on Sept. 28, 2025, against a National Assembly Act amendment tabled the previous day, according to reports. The bill adjusts standing committee names and jurisdictions to match ministry restructuring under the revised Government Organization Act, chiefly renaming three committees — Strategy and Finance, Environment and Labor, and Gender Equality and Family — it was reported.
- According to reports, the People Power Party continued the filibuster, saying that if the government reorganization bill took effect, it was only a matter of time before the country sank into a populist dictatorship like Venezuela, and Rep. Park criticized the reorganized structure as "creating a Frankenstein-like, monstrous organization." The Democratic Party forced an end to the filibuster after 24 hours and passed the bill the same day, reports said.

- "Populist dictatorship" and "the very presence of U.S. Forces Korea is nuclear deterrence" are claims and assessments by Rep. Park and the People Power Party.
