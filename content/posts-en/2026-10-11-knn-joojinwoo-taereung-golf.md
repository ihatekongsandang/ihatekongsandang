This post is based on the original caption and the reel's thumbnail (a news reel by broadcaster KNN). Because the thumbnail includes wording such as "shocking revelation," we did not use the original image.

## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- It posts video of Rep. Joo Jin-woo's press conference under the title "'Imperial golf at Taereung, even a clubhouse' — Joo Jin-woo holds urgent press conference with 'shocking revelation.'" The thumbnail reads "'Lee played golf during the Daegu accident.'"

## What reports confirm

- At a press conference at the National Assembly on Oct. 11, People Power Party Rep. Joo Jin-woo (주진우) reportedly said, "I received a tip that President Lee Jae-myung played imperial golf at Taereung CC on Oct. 10 and opened the presidential clubhouse and stayed there," released photos and video of the presidential motorcade and security vehicles parked in front of the clubhouse, and demanded that the president disclose "minute by minute, second by second" how the president responded to the ceiling collapse at a Daegu shopping building that day.
- In a statement, the presidential office's spokesperson's office reportedly said "the president visited Taereung golf course with family yesterday (Oct. 10)" and "was briefed on site about the shopping-building ceiling collapse in Daegu," then spoke directly with the acting national police commissioner about casualties and ordered relevant agencies to do their utmost to rescue people and recover swiftly and safely, adding, "We express regret that the president's private, undisclosed schedule is being used for political attacks." It was the first time the presidential office acknowledged a golf outing amid the president's golf controversies, according to reports.

## What we left out of the summary

- "Imperial golf" and "imperial clubhouse" are Rep. Joo's words, and the claim that the clubhouse opens only when the president visits was reported as Joo's assertion. The presidential office's statement said the president "visited Taereung golf course with family," which some outlets reported as an admission that the president played golf.
- Other allegations Joo raised, such as golfing during an earlier disappearance of navy sailors and the use of special-activity funds, were not checked in this post.
