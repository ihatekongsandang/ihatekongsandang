This post is based on an exclusive Dong-A Ilbo report. The report cites an anonymous senior government source; as of writing, we found no official statement from the Ministry of National Defense, the Joint Chiefs of Staff or the National Intelligence Service.

## What reports confirm

- Dong-A Ilbo reported that intelligence authorities had reportedly detected signs that North Korea had continued infiltration drills mobilizing submarines and covert-operation mother ships in areas including Nampo. A senior government source reportedly said on Oct. 27, "I understand intelligence authorities are watching the trend of North Korea continuing such drills on the occasion of its summer training."
- The article explains that a mother ship carries agents, small submarines and infiltration equipment and deploys or retrieves them in target waters.
- Intelligence authorities reportedly assess that the trend is tied to North Korea's moves to expand its Reconnaissance General Bureau into a "Reconnaissance and Intelligence General Bureau." The article says Kim Jong Un (김정은) signed an order on "important military measures" at an enlarged meeting of the Workers' Party Central Military Commission in July, where strengthening that bureau was mentioned.

## What we left out of the summary

- The scale, timing and frequency of the drills are not given in the article, so we did not state them. The view that the drills stem from instructions to step up operations against the South under the "two hostile states" doctrine is analysis reported in the article and was left out of the factual summary. We did not find independent confirmation by other outlets.
