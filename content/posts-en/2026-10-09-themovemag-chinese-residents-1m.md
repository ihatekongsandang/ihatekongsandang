This post is based on the original caption and its 11 images. Several of the original images are **composite or staged images** — Chinese President Xi Jinping standing on Gangnam-daero in Seoul, voting at a South Korean polling station and dancing in front of Bucheon Station, and President Lee Jae-myung in a meeting — so we used a card made by this site instead of the original images.

## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- It says Chinese residents in South Korea (including ethnic Korean Chinese) numbered about 1.02 million as of July 2026; that foreigners 18 or older who have held permanent residency for three years can vote in local elections, with a record 151,532 foreign voters this year, about 81% of them Chinese nationals according to 2025 data.
- It says Chinese regional health insurance subscribers paid 338 billion won in premiums and received 471.2 billion won in benefits in 2024, a 133.2 billion won deficit; that of 113.4 billion won in unemployment benefits paid to foreigners in 2025, about 89.5 billion won (78.9%) went to ethnic Korean Chinese and Chinese nationals; and that Chinese nationals owned about 61,000 homes at the end of 2025, 56.8% of foreign-owned homes. It adds that health insurance is in surplus when workplace subscribers are included and that unemployment benefits go to those who paid premiums and qualify, and argues it is time to discuss standards for foreigners' rights and reciprocity.

## What reports confirm

- Chinese residents in Korea (including ethnic Korean Chinese) numbered about 1.02 million as of July 2026, it was reported. Foreign voters in the June 3 local elections totaled a record 151,532, and of about 140,000 foreign permanent residents eligible to vote in local elections as of end-January 2025, about 110,000 (81%) were Chinese nationals, according to reports.
- In 2024, Chinese regional health insurance subscribers received 471.2 billion won in benefits against 338 billion won in premiums, a 133.2 billion won deficit, but all Chinese subscribers together paid 936.9 billion won and received 931.4 billion won, a 5.5 billion won surplus, it was reported.
- Unemployment benefits paid to foreigners in 2025 reached a record 113.399 billion won, of which 89.482 billion won (78.9%) went to ethnic Korean Chinese (73.8 billion) and Chinese nationals (15.6 billion), according to reports. At the same time, ethnic Korean Chinese and Chinese nationals made up 25.9% of foreign employment insurance subscribers.
- As of December 2025, foreigners owned 108,231 homes in Korea, about 61,000 (56.7–56.8%) of them held by Chinese nationals, roughly 0.55% of all homes, it was reported.

## What we left out of the summary

- The original image's "133.2 billion won health insurance deficit from Chinese subscribers a year" covers regional subscribers only; all Chinese subscribers together ran a surplus (the original caption makes the same point). The 89.5 billion won in "unemployment benefits paid to Chinese" includes ethnic Korean Chinese.
- We could not confirm the original image's claim that the government designated 37 overseas Korean residence support centers nationwide this year and funded them for the first time, or the extension of the metropolitan-area foreigner land-transaction permit zones to 2027.
