## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- The first screen reads, "Fight North Korea instead."

## What reports confirm

- At a plenary meeting of the National Assembly's Special Committee on Budget and Accounts on Nov. 11, People Power Party lawmaker Na Kyung-won (나경원) raised the Defense Ministry investigation headquarters' questioning of soldiers deployed during the Dec. 3 martial law and asked Defense Minister Kang Shin-chul (강신철), "Is our country a Tang dynasty army?" Kang reportedly replied that a lawmaker should not call the Korean armed forces a "Tang dynasty army," "and publicly at that," and asked her to withdraw the remark (Korea Economic Daily). The two also reportedly clashed over Kang's past remark about trusting U.S. forces and the postponement of the Security Consultative Meeting (SCM) (Financial News).
- The exchange continued after midnight in a session renumbered for the new day, and Kim Tae-kyu (김태규) reportedly told Kang that his "patience is very low," adding, "That fight is with North Korea," and "You are not fighting the opposition lawmakers who represent the people" (Korea Economic Daily). Freezine News reported the same remarks as saying someone "who leads an army of 500,000" has "very low patience," and that "you fight well, but that fight is for North Korea."
- At the same meeting, Democratic Party lawmaker Lee Ki-heon (이기헌) criticized Na, saying "Korea's military is not a Tang dynasty army," and as shouting broke out, committee chair Lee Kwang-jae (이광재) called a recess, saying "a cooling-off period seems necessary," according to reports (Korea Economic Daily).
- At the Nov. 12 meeting, when the previous day's dispute came up, Kang reportedly said, "I will humbly accept the points made about me" (Korea Economic Daily).
