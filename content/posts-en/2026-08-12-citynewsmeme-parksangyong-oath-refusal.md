This post is based on the original caption posted on Aug. 12, 2026; the video content was not reviewed.

## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- It says Prosecutor Park Sang-yong (박상용) calmly refused the witness oath at a parliamentary investigation hearing, in line with rights guaranteed by the Constitution and the law, and left the room.
- It regards the "allegations of coaxing testimony with a 'salmon and liquor party'" from the Ssangbangwool North Korea remittance investigation as an unfair political attack, and says it supports his principled stance in upholding the privilege against self-incrimination.

## What reports confirm

- Prosecutor Park reportedly refused the witness oath at the April 3, 2026, agency briefing of the parliamentary investigation into "allegations of fabricated indictments by politicized prosecutors under the Yoon Suk Yeol government," and again at the April 14 hearing on the North Korea remittance case, saying, "The special counsel must not withdraw the indictment against President Lee Jae-myung."
- Seo Young-kyo (서영교), chair of the special parliamentary investigation committee, reportedly said, "Isn't the most important witness refusing the oath a sign that he has decided to commit perjury?" and demanded a written explanation, and when Park refused, ordered him to leave.
- People Power Party Rep. Yoon Sang-hyun (윤상현) reportedly said a witness may refuse the oath under the Act on Testimony and Appraisal before the National Assembly if there is a risk of criminal prosecution, noting that Park has been reported to authorities, suspended from duty and banned from leaving the country, and Rep. Na Kyung-won (나경원) also reportedly argued that "he has the legal right to refuse the oath." Democratic Party Rep. Jeon Yong-gi (전용기) criticized Park as "the boy who cried wolf."
- It was reported that Park is facing allegations of coaxing testimony (the "salmon and liquor party") from former Gyeonggi Vice Governor for Peace Lee Hwa-young (이화영), and that in May the Justice Ministry extended Park's suspension from duty indefinitely, until a separate order is issued.

## What we left out of the summary

- This post does not judge whether the allegations of coaxing testimony are true. Related disciplinary and investigation outcomes will be reflected as they are confirmed.
