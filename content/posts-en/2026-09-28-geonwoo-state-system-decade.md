## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- It lists, in order: the 2017 scrapping of plans for six new nuclear reactors (the Shin-Kori 5 and 6 reactors resumed after public deliberation), the 2018 dissolution of the Defense Security Command (DSC) and launch of the Defense Security Support Command, the 2020 revision of the National Intelligence Service Act deciding to transfer counter-espionage investigation authority to police (effective 2024), the 2021 adjustment of prosecution-police investigative authority, the decision to dismantle and open the Four Major Rivers weirs, and the 2022 passage of the bills stripping prosecutors of investigative authority (known in Korean as "geomsu wanbak").
- It states that in 2026, the Prosecutors' Office will be abolished on Oct. 2 and split into a Serious Crimes Investigation Agency and a Public Prosecution Agency, and that the dissolution of the Defense Counterintelligence Command and the merger of the Army, Navy and Air Force academies are also being pursued.
- It says People Power Party Rep. Kim Tae-gyu (김태규) criticized the Democratic Party, saying "you've wrecked the national system like that," and argues that the reality of core systems being shaken with every change in administration should be watched closely.

## What reports confirm

- The revised Criminal Procedure Act abolishing prosecutors' supplementary investigation authority reportedly passed the National Assembly on July 31, 2026, and is tied to the Oct. 2 abolition of the Prosecutors' Office and the launch of the Public Prosecution Agency and the Serious Crimes Investigation Agency.
- The Defense Ministry reportedly announced on June 10, 2026 that it would dissolve the Defense Counterintelligence Command after 49 years, splitting its counterintelligence, security and investigative functions among new organizations, with the new structure to be completed by the end of July. Functions that had been used as tools for political interference, such as so-called "trend inquiries" (information-gathering on individuals), were abolished.
- The Defense Ministry is reportedly pushing ahead at speed with a plan to build a unified "Armed Forces Academy," merging the Army, Navy and Air Force academies, in Daejeon around 2028; reports said localities with the existing academies pushed back, calling it a "unilateral push."
