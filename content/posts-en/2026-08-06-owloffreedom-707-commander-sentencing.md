This post is based on the original caption and first image. The original was posted right after the closing hearing (Aug. 6) and said "the verdict will be on the 27th"; this post adds the later verdict. The first image's "President Yoon was right, martial law is not illegal" is a summary not found verbatim in reports.

## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- It says former 707th Special Mission Group commander K stated at the first-instance closing hearing that "martial law was a power held by former President Yoon Suk Yeol and cannot be illegal," and that the special counsel sought 18 years for K, 15 years for former 1st Airborne Brigade commander Lee Sang-hyun (이상현) and 12 years for former Defense Counterintelligence Command investigations chief Kim Dae-woo (김대우), rebuking them for "mobilizing elite troops as shock troops to violate democracy." It says K asked the court to "restore the honor of wronged soldiers."
- The caption ends by assessing that "this is where criticism arises that the trials of military officers are taking on a political character."

## What reports confirm

- At the closing hearing before the Seoul Central District Court's Criminal Agreement Division 37-2 on July 28, the insurrection special counsel team led by Cho Eun-suk sought 18 years for the former 707th commander K, charged with engaging in critical duties of insurrection, 15 years for Lee and 12 years for Kim Dae-woo, among others, it was reported. The counsel reportedly likened them to "enforcers who, on the boss's orders, led gang members to commit violence."
- On Aug. 27, the court sentenced the former 707th commander K to 12 years and took the former 707th commander K into custody in court; Lee received 10 years and Kim Dae-woo 5 years, while former Defense Ministry investigation headquarters chief Park Heon-su (박헌수) and former Defense Intelligence Command planning chief K were acquitted, it was reported. Explaining the sentence, the court reportedly said, in effect, that the former 707th commander K showed no remorse, claiming to have followed a superior's lawful orders, and distorted the facts; the former 707th commander K appealed the same day, according to reports.

## What we left out of the summary

- We did not find verbatim in the reports we checked the closing-hearing remarks the original attributes to the former 707th commander K ("martial law was a power held by former President Yoon Suk Yeol and cannot be illegal," "restore the honor of wronged soldiers") or the special counsel's phrase "shock troops to violate democracy."
- "Criticism that the trials are taking on a political character" is the poster's assessment.
