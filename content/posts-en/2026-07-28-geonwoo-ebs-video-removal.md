This post is based on the original caption; the video content was not reviewed.

## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- There are suspicions that a video explaining the principle that printing too much money lowers the value of currency and raises prices was taken down because it was perceived as critical of the government, and EBS should transparently explain what happened.

## What reports confirm

- The video at issue was reportedly Part 3 of the EBS documentary "The Face of Money," aired in April 2024 under the Yoon Suk Yeol administration, covering money issuance and inflation after COVID-19.
- EBS reportedly deleted the video with the thumbnail "Why I Get Poorer When the Government Hands Out Money," replaced it with an edited version and eventually made it private.
- EBS reportedly said, "It is content whose full video may not be uploaded because of a licensing contract signed with an external platform company in 2026, but the full video had been uploaded, so we re-uploaded it as a shortened clip," and explained the reason for making it private: "Regardless of the program's content, comments and community posts misreading the word 'government' as the South Korean government were found, so we made it private."
- Some civic groups reportedly criticized EBS for not sufficiently explaining who decided to make it private and on what criteria.

## What we left out of the summary

The suspicion that the government intervened in the video's removal has only been raised, and we found no reports supporting any intervention.
