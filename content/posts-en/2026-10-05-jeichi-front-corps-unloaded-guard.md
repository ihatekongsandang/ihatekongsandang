## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- Under a "breaking news" banner, it says that at a time when North Korean troops have crossed the Military Demarcation Line some 200 times even after the mine blast, it has been uncovered that every frontline corps from Ganghwa Island to the east coast stood guard without live ammunition.
- It says that at the same moment President Lee Jae-myung was posting on X about the abolition of the Prosecution Service, forced sales of farmland and confiscating the property of pro-Japanese collaborators, criticizes that "even as national security collapses, all they think about is extracting citizens' taxes and property," and urges people to share it.

## What reports confirm

- The defense ministry inspected how weapons and ammunition were handled by the Army's frontline units and the Marine Corps' guard units in August, and besides the Army's 1st Corps, cases of "unloaded" operation — personal or crew-served weapons without ammunition inserted during guard duty at GP (guard posts) and GOP (general outposts) — were reportedly also found in the 2nd, 3rd and 5th Corps and the 2nd Marine Division, according to reports on Oct. 5 citing the office of People Power Party Rep. Kang Sun-young (강선영) of the National Assembly's defense committee and others.
- By unit, the 2nd Corps reportedly operated K16 machine guns at GPs and K15 and K6 machine guns at GOPs unloaded; the 3rd Corps some crew-served weapons for about a month from July, citing a "combat experiment," plus personal weapons at GPs of its subordinate 22nd Division; the 5th Corps GP and GOP weapons other than K2 rifles; and the 2nd Marine Division personal and crew-served weapons during coastal and riverside guard duty.
- The 1st Corps reportedly changed its guidelines from the first half of 2026 so that ammunition for K6 heavy machine guns and K4 grenade launchers at GPs and GOPs was kept in boxes alongside rather than loaded (after an accidental K6 round fired toward the North in May last year), and the Joint Chiefs of Staff was reportedly not informed, according to reports on July 31.
- The ministry reportedly decided to issue institutional warnings to the Ground Operations Command, the 1st and 2nd Corps and the 3rd Corps' 22nd Division and a corrective measure to the JCS, while the 5th Corps was excluded from warnings because it went through procedures to revise its own guidelines, and no individual commanders will be disciplined. The ministry reportedly said the warnings were "so that the system can be improved."

## What we left out of the summary

- **"Without live ammunition"**: What was reported is "unloaded" operation — ammunition carried or kept in boxes but not inserted into the weapons. It does not mean there was no ammunition.
- **"Every frontline corps"**: Chosun Ilbo (조선일보) reported in an Oct. 5 exclusive that "every frontline corps of our military, from Ganghwa Island to the east coast, had cases of 'empty-gun guard duty,'" with "every corps" referring to the Army's 1st, 2nd, 3rd and 5th Corps, which handle GP and GOP guard duty on the front (plus the 2nd Marine Division). The original's wording is consistent with reports. However, the weapons and periods differ by unit, and the ministry reportedly said "the timing of changes to how ammunition was carried was decided by each corps and differs in every case." (Corrected Oct. 7: when first posted, this item wrongly suggested the original's wording differed from reports.)
- **"North Korean troops crossed 200 times"**: Defense intelligence assessed about 50 crossings in August–September; "over 200" was reported as an active-duty officer's claim with no counting period given (see the related post).
- We did not check the president's X posts mentioned in the original against the original posts. The assessment that "all they think about is extracting taxes and property" is the poster's opinion.
