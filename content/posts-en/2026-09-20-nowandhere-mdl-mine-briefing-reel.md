This post is based on the reel's caption (the video was not watched), and it may differ from the full video. The caption is written by the poster in a news format, and its last paragraph is the poster's commentary.

## Caption vs. reports

- The caption identifies the speaker as the "Defense Ministry spokesperson," but according to reports, the speaker was Jang Do-young (장도영), director of public affairs at the Joint Chiefs of Staff (JCS), who answered questions at the Defense Ministry's regular briefing on Sept. 14 (Herald Economy, Tongil News (통일뉴스)).
- The remark "We have identified some terrain changes and are consulting closely with the UN Command on the possibility that mines were laid" is confirmed by reports (Herald Economy, Tongil News).
- The caption's "cannot agree with the judgment of a failed response" was reportedly an answer — "I cannot agree with that judgment" — to the question of whether it could be judged that the existing principled response had failed (Tongil News).
- The caption's expressions "We have never directly said mines were laid," "artificial terrain changes" and "it is inappropriate to jump to conclusions" could not be found in the reports above. The reported expression is "For security reasons, we cannot confirm whether there are six places where mines were laid" (Herald Economy, Tongil News).

## Background and later reports

- At the National Assembly's interpellation session on Sept. 10, it was reportedly pointed out that six sites, including a minefield created by North Korean forces in the 25th Division's area of responsibility, had crossed the Military Demarcation Line by about 10–15 m (citing remarks by Rep. Yu Yong-weon (유용원) in a Newspim article of Sept. 21, 2026).
- After the reel was posted, on Sept. 21, three career service members were reportedly injured in an unidentified explosion during a DMZ operation by the Army's 25th Division, and Rep. Yu Yong-weon reportedly said the blast site was near a minefield created by North Korean forces and that whoever laid the mines must be identified (Newspim, Herald Economy, Sept. 21, 2026). Reports said it had not been confirmed whether the explosive was laid by North Korean forces.
