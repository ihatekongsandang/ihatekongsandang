This post is based on the original caption and thumbnail; the video itself was not reviewed. Developments in the farmland survey are summarized in the [farmland survey post](/post/2026-09-27-geonwoo-farmland-survey).

## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- It asks, "Take farmland, then Chinese solar?" and, with hashtags such as "withdraw the farmland survey entirely," "Chinese solar," "xie xie again" and "start with the presidential office," criticizes the government's farmland policy and its mention of solar power.
- The thumbnail uses the text "Seize farmland for Chinese solar" with a composite image of President Lee shaking hands against a Chinese flag and solar panels.

## What reports confirm

- On Sept. 29, President Lee Jae-myung reportedly said, "Using farmland for 'solar farming' might even become a trend."
- On Sept. 30, People Power Party leader Jang Dong-hyuk (장동혁) reportedly criticized the remark, saying, "It is hard to understand what talking about solar power with officials has to do with 'land to the tiller'" and "We cannot help but doubt the sincerity of the farmland survey."

## What we left out of the summary

- That the farmland survey is a policy to "take" farmland, and that solar on farmland would be filled with "Chinese-made" panels, are the poster's claims. We found no reports that the government plans to bring in Chinese panels.
- The handshake in the thumbnail is composited onto a Chinese flag and panel background; we did not verify the original setting.
