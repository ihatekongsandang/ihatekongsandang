The first scene was archival footage of crowds and vehicles from 1980, bearing the label "Seoul Economic TV" (서울경제TV). Related post (may be available only in Korean): [Rep. Lee Jin-sook (이진숙) calls for disclosure of May 18 merit records](/post/2026-09-13-geonwoo-leejinsook-518-merit-disclosure).

## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- Saying "the May 18 Pandora's box has been opened," it claims that protesters seized an armored vehicle, ran down Gwangju citizens with it and pinned the blame on the martial law troops.

## What reports confirm

- Regarding the death of Pfc. Kwon of the martial law troops (11th Airborne Brigade), who was crushed under an armored vehicle on May 21, 1980, former President Chun Doo-hwan (전두환) wrote in his memoir that he "was crushed to death under a protesters' armored vehicle." In September 2022, the Gwangju High Court reportedly found 51 passages, including this account, to be defamation through false facts and ordered their deletion and compensation, basing its judgment on testimony that Pfc. Kwon was crushed to death under a martial law troops' armored vehicle that was reversing.
- In February 2026, the Supreme Court reportedly finalized the victory of May 18 merit holders and organizations in this memoir case.
- In its 2024 comprehensive report, the May 18 Democratization Movement Truth Commission reportedly noted that statements about Pfc. Kwon's death conflict, but, citing the Gwangju High Court ruling, concluded that it was an accident caused by a martial law troops' armored vehicle.

## What we left out of the summary

- From the text alone, it is not clear whether the original means that Gwangju citizens or martial law soldiers died because of a protesters' armored vehicle. The fact that protesters secured and drove vehicles, including armored vehicles, in May 1980 is a separate matter from whose vehicle caused a particular death, and we found no report or ruling supporting the specific incident the original points to.
