## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- It says that President Lee Jae-myung, when ordering a full farmland survey at a February Cabinet meeting, said that even a sale order should be considered for land bought for farming but left idle.
- It says the government explains the purpose is to grasp land-use status and improve the system rather than force disposal, but points out that the Farmland Bank's purchase scale grew from 5,230 hectares and 1.8077 trillion won in 2026 to 6,800 hectares and 2.2617 trillion won in the government's 2027 budget proposal.
- It notes that owners of farmland under a disposal order can request the Farmland Bank buy it, and that a recent revision to the Farmland Act made mandatory purchase, within budget limits, applicable under certain conditions, criticizing this by asking, "unless this is a communist state, how far does the government intend to control farmers' land?"

## What reports confirm

- Under the Agriculture Ministry's 2027 budget proposal (22.2215 trillion won), the farmland supplied to young farmers and others is reportedly set to grow from 5,230 hectares this year to 6,800 hectares next year, and the related budget — including purchasing farmland for public lease and lease-then-sell arrangements — is reportedly set to rise by 454 billion won, from 1.8077 trillion won to 2.2617 trillion won. Reports describe this figure as the scale of "farmland supplied to young farmers and others," which differs from the original's description of it as the "purchase scale."
- Under the current Farmland Act, an owner of farmland under a disposal order can request that the Korea Rural Community Corporation (the Farmland Bank) purchase it.
- People Power Party leader Jang Dong-hyuk reportedly criticized the full farmland survey as a "communist idea" at a Sept. 27 press conference and demanded it be halted.

## What we left out of the summary

- The original's claim of "mandatory purchase under a recent Farmland Act revision" is presented here only as the original's claim, since we could not confirm the specific revised provision and its effective date from reports.
