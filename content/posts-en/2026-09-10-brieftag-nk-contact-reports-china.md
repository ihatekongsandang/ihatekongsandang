## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- It relays that reports of contact with North Korean residents in China rose ninefold, from 5 in January–June 2025 to 45 from the day the government took office (June 4) to the end of the year.
- It says this appears to be the effect of Unification Minister Chung Dong-young (정동영) scrapping internal guidelines that barred accepting reports of contact with members of North Korean agencies conducting operations against the South, among others, and notes that the People Power Party voiced concern about a weakened response to such operations.

## What reports confirm

- Reports say that, according to the Unification Ministry's "status of advance and post-contact reports of contact with North Korean residents" obtained by the office of People Power Party Rep. Park Choong-kwon (박충권), reports of contact with North Korean residents in China numbered 5 in January–June 2025, 45 from June 4 to the end of the year and 41 in January–July 2026, totaling 86, about 1.7 times the 50 reported during the previous government (May 2022–June 2025).
- Minister Chung reportedly scrapped, soon after taking office, the "guidelines for handling reports of contact with North Korean residents" created in 2023, which reportedly required refusing to accept a report when, for example, the applicant was a member of an organization benefiting the enemy or an anti-state organization, or the North Korean counterpart belonged to an agency conducting operations against the South. Minister Chung has said he would revitalize inter-Korean relations by expanding civilian exchanges.
- Since the Lee Jae-myung government took office, the ministry has reportedly refused an advance contact report in one case, compared with 10 under the Yoon Suk Yeol (윤석열) government. Rep. Park criticized the scrapping of the guidelines as opening the way for North Korean operations against the South.

## What we left out of the summary

- The thumbnail's "OK to meet North Korean spies in China" is the poster's wording; it has not been confirmed that any reported contact was with an operative.
