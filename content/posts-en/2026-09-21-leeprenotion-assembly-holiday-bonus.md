This post is based on the original text and the first image. The original image is a card that marks the item "holiday-leave allowance 8,495,880 won (60% of the general allowance each for Seollal and Chuseok)" in a table of lawmakers' allowances and reads "Lawmakers' holiday leave allowance: 8.49 million won."

## What reports confirm

- Under the "standards for paying lawmakers' allowances," lawmakers reportedly receive 60% of their monthly general allowance (7,327,600 won in 2026) as a holiday-leave allowance for Seollal (Lunar New Year) and for Chuseok. The 2026 Chuseok holiday allowance was reportedly about 4.4 million won per person, or about 8.79 million won a year.
- Reports made the comparison that the allowance for a single holiday is about 650,000 won more than the average monthly income of wage workers (3.75 million won) and about 6.6 times the average holiday bonus per employee at small and medium-sized companies (665,000 won).
- People Power Party Rep. Kim Mi-ae (김미애) reportedly donated her entire Seollal and Chuseok holiday allowances this year.

## What we left out of the summary

The "8.49 million won" in the original image differs from the 2026 figure in reports (about 8.79 million won). We could not confirm which year's allowance table the image used.
