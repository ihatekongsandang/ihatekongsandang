This post is based on the original caption; the video content was not reviewed. A video of the same day's Gwangju march by another user (@jung__khj) also shows a column of people holding pickets marching along a road at night, led by police.

## What the original post reports (the poster's account)

- On Saturday, Sept. 12, 2026, citizens in Gwangju denouncing President Lee Jae-myung and demanding his trial marched through the streets holding Taegeukgi flags and U.S. flags, chanting, "Lee Jae-myung, just go stand trial."
- There were also pickets at the scene reading, "A criminal as president?"
- The poster claimed that such rallies are not sufficiently covered by the major media.

## What reports confirm

- During this period, rallies and remarks by politicians calling for President Lee's trial to resume reportedly continued in Seoul and elsewhere. However, we found no press reports covering the Sept. 12 Gwangju march itself.

## What we left out of the summary

"Demands for a new election over election fraud, and for same-day voting and hand counting," as relayed by the original, were conveyed as rally slogans; no National Election Commission or court finding, or press report, supporting election fraud was confirmed, so they were not included in the summary. We also could not confirm the number of participants or the organizing group.
