This post is based on a reel from the official account of KNN, a broadcaster in the Busan and South Gyeongsang region (caption and 12 video frames reviewed). Because the poster in question was confirmed to be fake, we did not reuse it and used a card made by this site instead. A similar case (may be available only in Korean): [Report on punishment over a fake police protest video](/post/2026-10-07-swingmag-fake-police-protest-sentence).

## What the original says (KNN report)

- It says a poster bearing the defense ministry logo over a Chinese flag background, reading "Troop shortage — let's solve it together / Chinese people can be South Korean soldiers too!", spread online, drawing reactions such as "How can we trust China with our national defense?" and "Military secrets won't be safe anymore."
- Explaining that sensitivity had risen after a soldier of Chinese origin leaked military secrets last year, it reports that checking the poster's authenticity found it to be a fake poster made with AI.

## What reports confirm

- KNN reported that the poster was not made by the defense ministry but was an AI-generated fake.
- On the military secrets leak case mentioned in the video, military prosecutors said in an indictment in May 2025 that an active-duty sergeant, detained and indicted for leaking military secrets about South Korea–U.S. combined exercises to a Chinese military intelligence organization, was born in China in 2003 to a South Korean father and a Chinese mother, according to reports.

## What we left out of the summary

- The KNN report did not say who first made and spread the poster, and we could not confirm a separate defense ministry statement for this post. The identity of the soldier in the leak case is not covered.
