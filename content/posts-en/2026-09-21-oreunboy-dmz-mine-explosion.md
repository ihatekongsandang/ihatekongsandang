This post is based on the original caption; the content of the one attached image was not reviewed.

## What reports confirm

- The JCS reportedly said that around 10:50 a.m. on Sept. 21, a blast presumed to be a mine occurred while a front-line Army 25th Division unit was conducting a search operation south of the Military Demarcation Line in the DMZ on the western front.
- Three officers were reportedly injured, two of them seriously; one had a foot amputated at the ankle and underwent surgery. The injured were reportedly transported to the Armed Forces Capital Hospital.
- Whether the mine was laid by North Korean forces had not been confirmed, and the JCS reportedly said it was "investigating with all possibilities open."
- The blast was reportedly the first DMZ mine explosion in 11 years, since 2015.
