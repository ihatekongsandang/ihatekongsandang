## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- It criticizes summoning Chief Justice Cho Hee-dae to the National Assembly audit yet again, and demands that Kim Hyun-ji (김현지), a presidential office director (실장), be summoned instead, saying she should be forced to attend if she does not appear.

## What reports confirm

- The National Assembly's Legislation and Judiciary Committee reportedly passed, at its Sept. 28 full committee meeting, a ruling-party-led motion requesting the attendance of witnesses and unsworn testifiers for the National Assembly audit, including Chief Justice Cho Hee-dae, with the People Power Party walking out before the vote. The Supreme Court audit is set for Oct. 6.
- Chief Justice Cho reportedly recommended presiding judge Son Bong-gi (손봉기) for a Supreme Court justice post in August, but the presidential office asked him to submit a new recommendation; on Sept. 22, he publicly refused, saying there was "no constitutional basis or grounds" to do so.
- The People Power Party reportedly said it would seek to summon director Kim and former Democratic Party political-coordination office head J as audit witnesses to hold them accountable for personnel-appointment failures, but reports said adopting the summons would not be easy given opposition from the majority Democratic Party. The People Power Party had also criticized Kim's job transfer ahead of last year's audit as "audit avoidance."
