## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- It says a past directive in which North Korea set "passing the prosecution reform bill" as the first-stage goal for the Chungbuk Comrades Association side in 2019 is drawing attention again with the passage on July 31, 2026, of the Criminal Procedure Act amendment abolishing supplementary investigation authority.
- At the same time, it adds that "no direct evidence has been confirmed that the current legislation was pursued in accordance with North Korean directives," and that the substance of the past directive and the validity of the current system should be verified separately.

## What reports confirm

- In May 2022, citing the prosecution's indictment, NewDaily reported that in November 2019 North Korea's Cultural Exchange Bureau directed the "Independent Unification Chungbuk Comrades Association" side to make passing reform bills, including the prosecution reform bill, and having progressive, democratic and reformist forces seize the political initiative its "first-stage goal." This was a report based on the indictment, made while the trial was under way.
- On March 13, 2025, the Supreme Court reportedly finalized a two-year prison sentence for the Chungbuk Comrades Association's chairman and five-year sentences each for its vice chairman and adviser. The charge of organizing a criminal group was found not guilty, on the grounds that with at most four members it was difficult to regard as a criminal organization. The remaining member's five-year sentence was also reportedly finalized in September 2025.
- Reports say that on July 31, 2026, the Criminal Procedure Act amendment abolishing prosecutors' supplementary investigation authority passed a plenary session of the National Assembly with 175 of the 178 members present voting in favor, and that the People Power Party did not take part in the vote.

## What we left out of the summary

- Detailed figures in the original caption, such as "some 70 communications via encrypted files in 2018–2021," come from 2022 reports based on the indictment, so we did not separately present them as facts established by the courts.
