This post is based on the original caption and title; the card image content was not reviewed. **The trial in this case is ongoing**, and the content below was reported as the prosecution's charges, not the court's findings.

## What reports confirm

- In March 2023, prosecutors reportedly arrested and indicted four people affiliated with the "Independent Unification People's Vanguard" (자주통일 민중전위, known as Jatong) on charges of violating the National Security Act in connection with the so-called "Changwon spy ring" case. They are reportedly accused of using Changwon and other parts of South Gyeongsang Province as a base, meeting North Korean agents abroad to receive operating funds and directives, and reporting on the domestic situation.
- Prosecutors reportedly stated in the indictment that, following North Korean directives, the defendants posed as members of conservative YouTube channels and used the Cheong Wa Dae national petition system to build public opinion for shutting down conservative media outlets.
- Prosecutors reportedly said, "It directed comment teams to infiltrate online spaces and directed opinion manipulation actively using new methods such as national petitions and candlelight rallies," adding that "North Korea's methods of operations against the South have evolved."
- The trial, which had stalled for more than three years since the indictment, reportedly held a pretrial hearing again on Sept. 8, 2026, at Criminal Division 4 of the Changwon District Court, for the first time in about a year.

## What we left out of the summary

The defendants are contesting the charges, and no first-instance ruling has been handed down yet. Under the presumption of innocence, the charges are not established facts.
