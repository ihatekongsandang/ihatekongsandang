This post is based on the original (English) caption and thumbnail (one video frame); the full video was not reviewed. The original was posted on Oct. 20, 2026, but the remark it covers dates from **October 2025**. The original thumbnail overlays text on a photo of the president's face, so we used a card made by this site.

## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- It says that in an October 2025 U.S. television interview, President Lee said of North Korea's missile launch that "Chairman Kim did test-fire a missile, but [Kim] held back very well for a long time."
- It says Lee hoped President Trump and Kim would meet and would "fully welcome" a surprise U.S.-North Korea meeting at the summit, though Lee called it "not very likely."
- It adds that the Korean YouTube channel that posted the clip criticized the remark, saying the ones really holding back are South Koreans.

## What reports confirm

- In a CNN interview released on Oct. 23, 2025, President Lee Jae-myung (이재명) said of North Korea's recent ballistic missile launch, "Chairman Kim Jong Un did test-fire a missile, but in my view [Kim] held back very well for a very long time," according to reports. Hankook Ilbo reported that the interview was recorded the previous afternoon (Oct. 22).
- North Korea had fired what was presumed to be a Hwasong-11Ma hypersonic ballistic missile into the East Sea that morning, its first ballistic missile launch in 167 days since May 8 that year and the first since Lee took office, it was reported.
- On the possibility of Trump and Kim meeting on the sidelines of APEC in Gyeongju, Lee reportedly said "I hope they meet and talk," and that "though it is not very likely, if the U.S. and North Korea could meet suddenly, I would fully welcome and actively support it." Kyunghyang Shinmun reported that the remark was made in a joking tone in reply to a question suggesting it would not be the last launch.

## What we left out of the summary

- Derogatory wording in the YouTube channel's assessment relayed by the original is not reproduced. We did not review the YouTube video the original cites as its source.
