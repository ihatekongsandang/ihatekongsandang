This post is based on the original caption and the first frame of the reel. The first frame shows faces of protesters and police officers along with the text "Jailed one after another for shouting 'fake police' / Now protest and you go straight to prison," so we used a card made by this site instead of the original image. The defendants are private individuals, so their names are not given.

## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- The caption reports that three men who followed a police officer shouting "fake police" and surrounded and threatened the officer as a group were sentenced to six months in prison, and that two who had been free during trial were taken into custody in court. It says the officer wore a uniform and a vest with a name tag, carried a radio and stated their unit, rank and name, and that the officer refused to accept deposits of 15 million, 10 million and 10 million won made by the defendants.
- The first frame reads "Olympic Park protest: people in their 20s sentenced / Jailed one after another for shouting 'fake police' / Now protest and you go straight to prison."

## What reports confirm

- On Oct. 6, a single judge of the Seoul Eastern District Court's Criminal Division 7 reportedly sentenced three men charged with special obstruction of the performance of official duties to six months in prison each, taking the two who had been free during trial into custody citing a flight risk; the other had already been tried in detention. Prosecutors had reportedly sought one year each at the Sept. 1 closing hearing.
- In June, at a protest near the ballot-counting site at Olympic Park in Songpa-gu, Seoul, they were reportedly charged with following an on-duty police officer shouting "fake police" and surrounding and threatening the officer together with other participants.
- The court reportedly noted that if they genuinely suspected impersonation they could have checked by calling 112 but did nothing, and that they acted on vague suspicion and speculation and showed no remorse while denying the offense. The defendants' deposits (10 million to 15 million won each) were reportedly given only limited weight because the officer refused to accept them.

## What we left out of the summary

- **"Jailed for shouting 'fake police'" and "protest and you go straight to prison"**: The reported conviction concerns not just the shouting but surrounding and threatening an officer (special obstruction of official duties), not punishment for joining a protest as such.
- **"People in their 20s"**: One defendant's age was reported differently by outlet — 35 (The Financial News, Seoul Economic Daily) and 25 (Seoul Shinmun, SBS). SBS reported the three as 25, 23 and 24.
- **"South Korea proven a communist state, going the same way as Hong Kong" (@jchive.log)**: This is the poster's assessment. The SBS headline shown in the video reads "Aren't you Chinese police?", but the same article's body says they were charged with shouting "fake police" at the officer.
- This is a first-instance ruling; whether an appeal was filed was not confirmed in the reports.
