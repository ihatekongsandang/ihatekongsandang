The content of the original's nine card images was not reviewed; this post is based only on the post's body text and the reports below. The poster describes the original as "a summary of public reports, including opinion."

## What reports confirm

- Forty-six former Army, Navy and Air Force chiefs of staff (18 Army, 13 Navy, 15 Air Force) reportedly issued a statement urging a review of the military academy merger (News1, Segye Ilbo, Aug. 6, 2026).
- In a Korea Gallup survey of 1,001 people aged 18 or older nationwide conducted Aug. 25–27, 58% reportedly answered that "it is better not to merge" the military academies (Hankook Kyungje TV (한국경제TV), Aug. 28, 2026).
- On the afternoon of Sept. 19, a nationwide mass rally to stop the closure of the military academies was reportedly held near Gyeongbokgung Station in Jongno-gu, Seoul (Newspim photo article, Sept. 19, 2026).

## Claims in the original left out of the summary

The original's "first time since the founding of the armed forces," "the president's 'coup' remark in August" and "the Defense Ministry's basic merger plan in July" could not be confirmed by the reports above, so they were not included in the summary.
