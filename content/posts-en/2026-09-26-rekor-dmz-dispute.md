This post is based on the original caption; the card image content was not reviewed. The original caption cites an MBC report (Sept. 26, 2026) as related coverage.

## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- It notes criticism that, at the time of the ROKS Cheonan sinking and the Yeonpyeong Island shelling too, some Democratic Party figures raised doubts about the investigation results or pushed a government-responsibility narrative.
- It argues that in a case where troops were injured, what matters is not blocking political strife but determining the cause, and that the possibility of North Korean responsibility should be pursued and clarified as well.

## What reports confirm

- Democratic Party chief spokesperson Park Sung-joon (박성준) reportedly said on Sept. 26 that "the People Power Party's use of security as a tool for political strife is going too far," and that "the government and military have never ruled out the possibility that North Korea laid the mine" and are "investigating with all possibilities open."
- The Democratic Party reportedly demanded that the People Power Party "immediately stop raising conspiracy theories that stoke security anxiety," saying the PPP had even raised "deference to Kim Jong-un," "cover-up," and "grounds for impeachment."
- People Power Party leader Jang Dong-hyuk (장동혁) reportedly criticized on Sept. 27 that "it is very likely a North Korean box mine that North Korean forces recently crossed the Military Demarcation Line to lay," and that "the military only began preliminary preparation work, not a full investigation, five days after the accident."

## What we left out of the summary

The military's official investigation result on the cause of the blast had not yet come out. The Democratic Party figures' remarks at the time of the Cheonan sinking and Yeonpyeong shelling were not covered in the source reports for this post and were not verified.
