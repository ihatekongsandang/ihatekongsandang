## What reports confirm

- On Sept. 21, three officers were reportedly injured in a blast presumed to be a mine during a 25th Division DMZ search operation.
- On the morning of Sept. 22, a captain assigned to 25th Division headquarters was reportedly found dead in his quarters. Unit members reportedly went to check on him after he failed to report for duty and found him there.

## How the two incidents relate

The deceased captain held a post involved in operational planning and similar duties at division headquarters and, according to reports, **was found not to be in a branch directly involved in the DMZ search operation**. Reports said the two cases appear unrelated.

No signs of forced entry or criminal involvement have been confirmed, and military authorities and the Gyeonggi Northern Provincial Police Agency have reportedly begun questioning unit personnel and others.
