The thumbnail carries the caption "All power comes from the Democratic Party" and the description "2026.07.20 Rep. Yoon Sang-hyun's filibuster against the second comprehensive special counsel extension bill."

## What reports confirm

- On July 20, 2026, the Democratic Party reportedly put before the plenary session an amendment to the special counsel law extending by 30 days the investigation period of the second comprehensive special counsel, which had been set to end on July 24, and increasing the number of dispatched public officials from 130 to 150.
- The People Power Party reportedly filed for a filibuster immediately, with Rep. Yoon Sang-hyun (윤상현) taking the floor as the first unlimited-debate speaker.
- Rep. Yoon reportedly said, "Since the special counsel system was introduced in 1999, there has been no precedent for extending the investigation period of an ongoing special counsel by amending the law," and "This amendment is an attempt to turn the special counsel into a permanent political power agency rather than a temporary exceptional body," adding, "After Supreme Court expansion, a four-tier trial system, the crime of distorting the law and the abolition of prosecutors' supplementary investigation authority, the Democratic Party now wants to extend even the special counsel indefinitely."
- The Democratic Party's position was reportedly that the investigation must be "wrapped up for certain."
