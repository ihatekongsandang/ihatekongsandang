## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- It calls the government a "'Jaemdemic' that brings disaster to everything it touches," criticizing that public safety, real estate and the rule of law are collapsing and that the separation of powers is being trampled to erase one person's crimes.
- It says the People Power Party promises to restore supplementary investigation powers; abolish inheritance and gift taxes between spouses and taxes on overtime; monitor the National Election Commission and restore the housing ladder; guarantee the independence of the National Pension Service; repay national debt with excess tax revenue; double veterans' honor allowances; and pursue Korea–U.S. nuclear sharing and the redeployment of tactical nuclear weapons.

## What reports confirm

- In his Sept. 8 speech as floor leader of a negotiating group, People Power Party floor leader Jung Jeom-sik (정점식) reportedly defined the current situation as an "era of secret burial" and proposed restoring supplementary investigation powers, fully abolishing inheritance and gift taxes between spouses, abolishing taxes on overtime income and at least doubling veterans' honor allowances, among other things.
- Floor leader Jung reportedly said the party would secure nuclear deterrence through Korea–U.S. nuclear sharing, the redeployment of tactical nuclear weapons to the Korean Peninsula and training for F-35s to carry tactical nuclear weapons, and also announced a push to fully abolish early voting.
- The amendment to the Criminal Procedure Act abolishing prosecutors' supplementary investigation authority passed the National Assembly on July 31 with the People Power Party not taking part in the vote.

## What we left out of the summary

- Expressions criticizing the government, such as "concealing evidence of crimes" and "pledge fraud," are the poster's assessments, so they were not treated as subjects of fact-checking.
