This post is based on the original caption and thumbnail; the video content was not reviewed. The original thumbnail's "50,000 Shincheonji members in the Democratic Party," "the Shincheonji involvement claim was true" and "did they take over the Democratic Party?" are more definitive than reports, so we used a card made by this site.

## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- It says the Democratic Party, through its party membership task force, found signs that at least 30,000 members of Shincheonji (the Shincheonji Church of Jesus) joined the party en masse in connection with the "Shincheonji involvement" claim raised during the Aug. 17 party convention, with the possibility of more than 50,000 also raised. It notes this would be about 1.9–3.2% of the party's roughly 1.55 million members at the time.
- It says the party is weighing how much to disclose because releasing the findings could reignite factional conflict, and that the key questions are the actual scale, how members joined and whether there was organized involvement.

## What reports confirm

- Citing a party official, reports said signs were found that more than 30,000 Shincheonji members joined the party collectively, possibly more than 50,000; the membership task force led by Supreme Council member Park Sun-won (박선원), working to clean up dual party membership, ghost members and involuntary enrollment, reportedly investigated this and reported to the Supreme Council.
- Party leader Kim Min-seok (김민석) reportedly said on the party's YouTube program on Oct. 14 that "it has been confirmed that there was a considerable degree of collective enrollment, and we have also accumulated quite meaningful testimony," and on whether there was organized interference in the party election, "there is testimony on that." However, on reports of at least 30,000, Kim reportedly said "it was estimated using one method we could verify, not through an investigation, and the reported number is not accurate," adding that whether to disclose the findings to lawmakers or request an investigation would be decided carefully.
- The pro-Jung Chung-rae faction pushed back. Supreme Council member Han Min-soo (한민수) reportedly said "what was shared orally in early October was only signs of enrollment from cross-checking membership lists" and "it was clearly said that organized voting was not confirmed either," and Rep. Jung Chung-rae (정청래) reportedly demanded that the findings be "disclosed as they are." The allegation arose when Kim, during the August convention, spoke of a "secret three-way alliance of anti-Lee, divisive and Shincheonji forces," according to reports.

## What we left out of the summary

- The enrollment figures (30,000–50,000) come from an unnamed official; Kim said they are "not accurate," and the findings have not been released. Organized voting interference has not been confirmed, with accounts differing even within the party. The thumbnail's "did they take over the Democratic Party?" is the poster's wording.
