This post is based on the original caption; the card image content was not reviewed. The original caption cites Chosun Ilbo (Sept. 28, 2026) and Seoul Economic Daily (Aug. 2, 2026) reports as its sources.

## What reports confirm

- The Sept. 21 blast reportedly occurred while a search route was being cleared ahead of a UN Command investigation, to check a suspected North Korean minefield thought to extend across the Military Demarcation Line (MDL).
- The Defense Ministry reportedly suspended 1st Corps commander Han Ki-sung (한기성) from duty on Aug. 3 over the "empty-gun guard duty" guideline controversy at front-line units on the western front and a controversy over an omitted drone-related report. The 1st Corps reportedly issued a guideline for GP/GOP personnel to keep crew-served weapons such as the K4 and K6 without loaded ammunition, keeping ammunition cans nearby instead.
- In an Aug. 2 column, Seoul Economic Daily reportedly cited military sources saying that, while serving as 25th Division commander, Han ignored orders the JCS had issued by wire and radio, reportedly because of concern that checking MDL marker signs inside the DMZ could result in casualties.

## What we left out of the summary

We could not find the full text of the Chosun Ilbo Sept. 28 report cited in the original ("North Korean minefield spotted last year, former division commander shelved order to check it") and so could not verify its contents. The claims that the former division commander avoided checking the minefield and that this led to the current accident are, at this stage, allegations attributed to military sources; there has been no explanation from the person concerned or an official investigation result.
