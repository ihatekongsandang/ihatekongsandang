## What reports confirm

- Before the South Korea–Bangladesh match in Group A of the men's hockey group stage at the 2026 Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games (Kakamigahara Green Stadium, Gifu Prefecture, Japan), North Korea's national anthem was reportedly played for about a minute instead of South Korea's national anthem.
- When South Korean players took their hands off their chests and pointed out the error to officials, the organizers reportedly turned off the North Korean anthem and played Bangladesh's, and South Korea's anthem was reportedly played only afterward, while the players were shaking hands on the referee's instructions, so the players went into the match without singing their anthem.
- The Korean Sport & Olympic Committee reportedly sent a letter of protest to the organizing committee in the name of chef de mission Lee Sang-hyun (이상현), demanding an explanation of what happened, an official apology and measures to prevent a recurrence.
- Two organizing committee officials reportedly visited the South Korean delegation's office on Sept. 19 and apologized for the anthem error.
