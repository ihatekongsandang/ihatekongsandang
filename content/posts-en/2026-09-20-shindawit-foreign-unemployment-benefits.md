## What reports confirm

- Unemployment benefits paid to foreigners in 2025 reportedly totaled about 113.4 billion won, exceeding 110 billion won for the first time and reaching a record high. They reportedly rose from the 75.9-billion-won range in 2022 to the 80.5-billion-won range in 2023 and the 91.7-billion-won range in 2024.
- Of this, the amount paid to ethnic Korean Chinese (about 73.8 billion won) and Chinese nationals (about 15.6 billion won) was reportedly about 89.5 billion won, or 78.9% of the total, and by number of recipients they reportedly accounted for 13,083, or 77.9% of all 16,789 foreign recipients.
- After those of Chinese origin, the largest numbers of recipients were reportedly from Vietnam, the United States, Japan, Taiwan and the Philippines, in that order.
- The number of foreigners enrolled in employment insurance has reportedly grown about 2.6-fold in four years, and reports cited analysis that the high share of recipients of Chinese origin is because many have stayed long-term and become part of the labor market.
