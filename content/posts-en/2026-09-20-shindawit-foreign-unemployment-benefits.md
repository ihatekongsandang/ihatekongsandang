An [Instagram post by @got_dem_catch](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdJa_20TYKN/) on the same subject (33.2 billion won in fraudulent unemployment benefits and foreign recipient statistics) is noted here instead of in a separate post. This post is based on the original caption; the image content was not reviewed.

## What reports confirm

- Unemployment benefits paid to foreigners in 2025 reportedly totaled about 113.4 billion won, exceeding 110 billion won for the first time and reaching a record high. They reportedly rose from the 75.9-billion-won range in 2022 to the 80.5-billion-won range in 2023 and the 91.7-billion-won range in 2024.
- Of this, the amount paid to ethnic Korean Chinese (about 73.8 billion won) and Chinese nationals (about 15.6 billion won) was reportedly about 89.5 billion won, or 78.9% of the total, and by number of recipients they reportedly accounted for 13,083, or 77.9% of all 16,789 foreign recipients.
- After those of Chinese origin, the largest numbers of recipients were reportedly from Vietnam, the United States, Japan, Taiwan and the Philippines, in that order.
- The number of foreigners enrolled in employment insurance has reportedly grown about 2.6-fold in four years, and reports cited analysis that the high share of recipients of Chinese origin is because many have stayed long-term and become part of the labor market.

## Checking the post noted here (@got_dem_catch)

- According to data the Ministry of Employment and Labor submitted to the office of People Power Party Rep. Kim Seung-soo (김승수), fraudulent unemployment benefits last year (2025) reportedly totaled 33.201 billion won, up 23.9% from 2022, with 25,109 cases detected and 2,162 repeat cases (6.172 billion won) since 2021.
- That post put foreign recipients "last year" at 12,658, including 8,103 ethnic Koreans from China and 1,740 Chinese nationals (about 78%), but this differs from the reported number of foreign recipients last year cited above (16,789), so we could not confirm which year its figures are for. The share of Chinese-origin recipients, about 78%, is similar in both.
- As that post notes, the 78% refers to all foreign recipients, including lawful ones, not to people who received benefits fraudulently.
