## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- It criticizes the Democratic Party for stressing a response to the DMZ mine blast while skipping the National Assembly Foreign Affairs and Unification Committee's inquiry into the blast and the repatriation of North Korean POWs.
- It says the Democratic Party, Foreign Minister Cho Hyun (조현) and Unification Minister Chung Dong-young (정동영) were absent from the committee's Sept. 30 full meeting.
- It concludes that "anyone who wants to talk about DMZ security should first show up responsibly for the Assembly inquiry."

## What reports confirm

- People Power Party Rep. Kim Dae-sik (김대식), the opposition's senior committee member, reportedly submitted a request on Sept. 29 to convene a full meeting, and the ruling party reportedly signaled it would not attend, saying the schedule had not been agreed and the annual audit was about to begin.
- The Sept. 30 full meeting was reportedly held by the People Power Party alone, without the Democratic Party's committee members; Foreign Minister Cho reportedly cited his work schedule and Unification Minister Chung an overseas trip. The meeting reportedly ended after about an hour.
- The Democratic Party's position was reportedly that a separate inquiry was not very necessary because the parliamentary audit was scheduled for Oct. 6–7.
- Committee chair Song Seok-jun (송석준) of the People Power Party reportedly said he "deeply regrets holding the meeting without sufficient consultation and agreement between the two parties' senior members," and criticized that "not a single government official who should take responsibility for this grave situation is here."

## What we left out of the summary

- The original's "all absent" reads as meaning all Democratic Party committee members were absent, and reports likewise say the party's members did not attend. Individual lawmakers' reasons for not attending were not confirmed in the reports.
