This post is based on the original text and one attached image. A post covering the same announcement: [JCS interim findings summary](/post/2026-09-28-pagememo-jcs-interim-result).

> 🔁 **Correction (2026-09-28)**: The first version of this post, based on that afternoon's reports (a briefing to the Defense Committee), said "the JCS's interim findings announcement has not been confirmed." Reports later that evening confirmed the JCS had announced its interim findings, and this has been corrected.

## What the original post claims

- It says the JCS's interim findings announcement stated the blast point was within a 10 m radius south of the MDL, that South Korean forces had never laid a mine there, that the mine was presumed to be brown in color, and that it was presumed North Korean forces crossed the MDL to lay it.

## What reports confirm

- The JCS reportedly announced its interim findings on Sept. 28, saying the blast point was about 10 m south of the Military Demarcation Line and that there was no record of South Korean mines having been laid in the area.
- The National Forensic Service's analysis reportedly found TNT and brownish foreign matter, but no metal fragments, on three items including the injured troops' ballistic helmets and vests, and JCS operations chief Yang Jin-hyeok (양진혁) reportedly explained, "It is not our mine and very likely a North Korean mine."
- One combat engineer reportedly testified to seeing a brown square object presumed to be a mine about 1 m from the first blast point, and the JCS reportedly assessed it as "a brown resin anti-personnel mine similar in shape to a North Korean box mine."
- The JCS reportedly said that if a North Korean mine is finally confirmed, "all responsibility lies with North Korea" and it "will implement appropriate and corresponding measures."

## What we left out of the summary

Reports say the JCS **has not yet concluded whether North Korean forces recently laid the mine directly at that spot or whether a mine from elsewhere was displaced there**. The original's "presumed North Korea crossed the MDL to lay it" goes further than what was announced. The site investigation had not yet concluded.
