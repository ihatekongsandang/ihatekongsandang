## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- It says a controversial bill has been introduced under which "cursing Chinese people could bring up to five years in prison," and criticizes it, asking what is happening under this administration.

## What reports confirm

- On Nov. 4, 2025, Democratic Party Rep. Yang Bu-nam (양부남), together with 11 lawmakers, sponsored a partial amendment to the Criminal Act punishing insults to, and defamation by false facts of, a specific country, the nationals of a specific country or a specific race. Yang reportedly cited insults and defamation at "anti-China rallies" on National Foundation Day as the background to the bill.
- The bill would reportedly punish defaming the honor of a specific country or race by stating false facts with up to five years in prison, among other penalties, and publicly insulting a specific country, its nationals or a race with up to one year in prison or imprisonment without labor, or a fine of up to 2 million won. In other words, the "up to five years in prison" is the false-fact defamation clause, and insult ("cursing") carries up to one year.
- The opposition criticized it as a "China protection act" that chills freedom of expression, and according to the bill information, as of September 2026 the bill is at the Legislation and Judiciary Committee review stage and has not been put to a plenary vote.

## What we left out of the summary

- The original writes, in effect, that "this administration" is making the law, but this bill was introduced by lawmakers, not the government.
- **@park_kwon_chun's "passed in the end"**: According to the National Assembly bill information (Open Assembly, as updated Oct. 10), the bill's status is "under review by the standing committee," with no plenary vote information. Being referred to and reviewed by a committee subcommittee is not "passage." As noted above, the "5 years in prison" is the false-fact defamation clause, while insult ("cursing") carries up to one year. We do not reproduce the swear word in the original text.
