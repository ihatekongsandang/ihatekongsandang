## What reports confirm

- According to material People Power Party Rep. Ahn Cheol-soo (안철수) obtained from the Ministry of Unification, the 2026 budget earmarked for relief aid and livelihood cooperation with North Korea is 667.61 billion won, and spending plans for three of its programs — relief aid, support for North Korean infants and others, and cooperation in agriculture, livestock, forestry and the environment, totaling 515.781 billion won (77.3%) — were reportedly still "undecided" as of September.
- The programs for which the ministry specified an expected executable amount reportedly total 994 million won, including 200 million won for research services and 794 million won for operating an integrated humanitarian aid management system.
- Reports say the government cut 237.844 billion won from the related budget in last year's second supplementary budget, and that this year's budget is 317.339 billion won (90.6%) larger than that revised plan. Government humanitarian aid actually delivered to North Korean residents last year was reportedly zero won.
- The ministry reportedly explained that it increased the budget for humanitarian cooperation projects to support its policy of peaceful coexistence on the Korean Peninsula.
