This post is based on the original caption and thumbnail posted on Aug. 25, 2026; the video content was not reviewed. According to the KNN video, the questioning took place at the National Assembly's Special Committee on Budget and Accounts. Related post (may be available only in Korean): [Key issues in the constitutional amendment](/post/2026-09-20-hagonolza-constitution-amendment-points).

## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- It relays that when People Power Party Rep. Kim Tae-gyu (김태규) asked her view on putting the Korean War's spirit of fighting for freedom in the constitutional preamble, Prime Minister Han Sung-sook (한성숙) answered, "It requires national consensus and the necessary procedures," and "The May 18 content is a matter on which national consensus has been formed across parties."
- It says that when Rep. Kim asked back, "Does that mean there is not yet consensus on the Korean War's spirit of fighting for freedom?", the prime minister answered, "It is not appropriate to answer a hypothetical situation," and "Putting May 18 in the Constitution is being pursued as national agenda item No. 1."
- It relays that Rep. Kim ended his questioning by saying, "The prime minister's answer can only be read as negative toward putting the spirit of the Korean War in the Constitution."

## What reports confirm

- KNN reported in a video on Aug. 26 the scene at the budget committee in which Rep. Kim asked, in effect, why May 18 was acceptable but the Korean War was not, and Prime Minister Han answered.
- Reports say a constitutional amendment putting the spirit of May 18 in the constitutional preamble was submitted to the National Assembly in April 2026, and that the Democratic Party urged the People Power Party to make a decision.

## What we left out of the summary

- The thumbnail presents "no consensus has formed on the Korean War's spirit of fighting for freedom" as if it were the prime minister's quoted words, but according to the original caption, it was Rep. Kim's rejoinder, and the prime minister said "it is not appropriate to answer a hypothetical situation." The title and summary of this post are based on the remarks as written in the caption.
