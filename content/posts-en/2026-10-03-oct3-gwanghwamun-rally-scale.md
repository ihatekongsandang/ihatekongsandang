After the Oct. 3 rallies, posts showing the same scenes appeared one after another from many accounts, so we have set them out together with the reported attendance. Claims that "the media did not report it" are checked separately in [National Foundation Day rally in Gwanghwamun — was the media silent?](/post/2026-10-04-ycherish-oct3-rally-media).

## What reports confirm

- **Gwanghwamun (Sejong-daero)**: The Liberty Unification Party, centered on Pastor Jeon Kwang-hoon (전광훈) of Sarang Jeil Church, reportedly held the "Oct. 3 Gwanghwamun National Rally for the Resignation of the Lee Jae-myung Government" on Sejong-daero from 1 p.m. on Oct. 3, with about 28,000 participants according to an unofficial police estimate. Participants reportedly demanded a full investigation into the DMZ mine blast, a halt to screenings of the film 'Assassin(s)', an end to visa-free entry for Chinese nationals, a halt to the merger of the military academies and the resignation of Defense Minister Kang Shin-chul (강신철), and marched toward Singyo intersection from 4 p.m.
- **Sungnyemun**: The People Power Party reportedly held a "National Action Rally to Judge the Lee Jae-myung Government" around Sungnyemun from 3 p.m., with about 9,000 attending according to an unofficial police estimate, reportedly its first large-scale street rally in downtown Seoul in a year. Conservative groups also reportedly held separate rallies around Seoul City Hall and Seoul Station.
- **Downtown conditions**: Rallies and marches held simultaneously around Sejong-daero, Sungnyemun and Bosingak reportedly caused continuing traffic congestion on major roads around Gwanghwamun and City Hall. The same day, a rally condemning Israel (about 1,000) was reportedly held at Bosingak and a Candlelight Action rally (5,000 registered) near Seocho Station.

## Photos and videos shared on social media

- From the afternoon of the rally into the next day, many Threads accounts posted photos and videos taken from above of crowds filling Sejong-daero and the Gwanghwamun area. One account posted a video of the Sejong-daero rally at around 4 p.m. on Oct. 3, and photos and videos posted by other accounts show the same location (around Sejong-daero and the Jongno 1-ga road sign).
- That different accounts shared similar scenes of the same location is consistent with reports that a large crowd gathered around Sejong-daero that day.

## What we left out of the summary

- For attendance, we used only the figures reported as unofficial police estimates. We found no reports supporting figures such as "200,000" cited by some posts, and the number of photos and videos on social media cannot by itself establish a headcount.
- We could not confirm the exact time each photo or video was taken. The photo above was also shared on Oct. 4, and the time it was taken is not shown in the original.
- One outlet (Hankook Ilbo) reported attendance at the People Power Party rally as "about 100," differing from other reports (about 9,000).
- Slogans about election fraud chanted at the rally are unverified claims and were not reproduced.
