This post is based on the original caption and one image.

## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- It says Kim Min-woong (김민웅), the older brother of Democratic Party leader Kim Min-seok (김민석), has argued for adopting Venezuelan-style justice and politics, for withdrawing U.S. forces and dissolving the ROK-U.S. alliance, and that "Korea's greatest enemy is the United States," and that he is a "sex-crime convict" whose conviction as a second-degree offender in the Park Won-soon (박원순) sexual assault case was finalized by the Supreme Court with a prison sentence.
- It says he pushed the conspiracy theory that the Cheonan was sunk by an internal explosion rather than by North Korea, stirred up fears about Fukushima treated water, and works with the Democratic Party and Lee Jae-myung as a speaker.
- It claims that the Democratic Party and President Lee working with Kim proves the party is also a pro-North, anti-American force pursuing communist dictatorship, calling it "anti-state forces."

## What reports confirm

- Former Prime Minister Kim Min-seok was reportedly elected Democratic Party leader at the party convention on Aug. 17, and Kim Min-woong, head of the civic group Candlelight Action, is reportedly his older brother.
- In October 2024, the Supreme Court reportedly upheld a sentence of one year in prison, suspended for two years, for former professor Kim Min-woong on charges of violating the Act on Special Cases Concerning the Punishment of Sexual Crimes (breach of confidentiality). He was charged with posting on social media, in December 2020, a letter written by the victim in the case involving former Seoul Mayor Park Won-soon, together with her real name.
- In July 2025, Kim reportedly caused controversy with social media posts such as "We must create an era in which Yankee imperialism no longer works. The first step is sending U.S. forces in Korea home" and "The ROK-U.S. alliance is a shackle around our neck."

## What we left out of the summary

- The card's "prison sentence" is inaccurate. The finalized sentence was a **suspended** prison term, and the offense was breaching confidentiality by disclosing the victim's identity under the sexual crimes act.
- We could not find reports directly supporting the claims that he argued for Venezuelan-style justice and politics, said "Korea's greatest enemy is the United States," claimed the Cheonan was sunk by an internal explosion, or campaigned on Fukushima treated water, so we did not summarize them.
- Labeling the entire Democratic Party a "force pursuing communist dictatorship" or "anti-state forces" on the basis of Kim's remarks and record is the poster's claim.
