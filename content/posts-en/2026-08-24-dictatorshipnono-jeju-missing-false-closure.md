This post is based on the original caption; the video content was not reviewed. The original was posted on Aug. 24, 2026, and the "arrest warrant hearing today (the 25th)" mentioned in the caption has since produced a result.

## What reports confirm

- A police officer in his 30s (rank of senior officer) on the missing-persons team at Jeju Seobu Police Station is reportedly suspected of receiving a missing-person report for a woman in her 30s on May 15, 2026, and closing the case by falsely recording that contact had been made, without confirming whether she was alive or where she was (charges including dereliction of duty and forging and using public electronic records). The missing woman was reportedly found dead in a palm grove in Hallim-eup, Jeju City, on Aug. 24, 101 days after the report.
- Reports say the officer was released after an emergency arrest because of procedural requirement issues, and that on Aug. 25 the Jeju District Court issued an arrest warrant, saying "a risk of flight and a risk of destroying evidence are recognized."
- Reports say the missing-person cases the officer closed numbered 298, or 28.4% of the total in that period, that the police launched a full review over the possibility of further harm, and that as of Aug. 31, 63 of them could not be confirmed within the management network and were being re-verified by type.
- The Chinese flag (Five-Star Red Flag) on a beach on Udo Island mentioned in the original was reportedly set up in July 2025 by an employee of a nearby café as a photo zone for Chinese tourists and removed a day later.

## What we left out of the summary

As the original itself notes, reports do not confirm any evidence that the deaths of missing people recently found in Jeju are connected crimes. The Udo Island Chinese flag controversy is separate from the missing-person case.
