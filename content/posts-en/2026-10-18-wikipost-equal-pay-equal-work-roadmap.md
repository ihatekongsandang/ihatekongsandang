This post is based on the original caption and its single thumbnail. Because the original thumbnail puts the poster's assessment — "The government will pay socialist wages / Doesn't care about productivity, only knows socialism" — over a photo of the president, we used a card made by this site.

## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- It says the Ministry of Employment and Labor is considering adding to the Labor Standards Act the principle of "equal pay for work of equal value," so that people doing work of the same value receive the same pay regardless of employment type or gender, and plans to announce a detailed roadmap in December.
- It lists challenges such as moving from seniority-based to job-based pay, unions' concern about wage cuts and expanding industry-level bargaining. The thumbnail reads "The government will pay socialist wages" and "Doesn't care about productivity, only knows socialism."

## What reports confirm

- The labor ministry is gathering expert opinions to announce a "roadmap for realizing equal pay for work of equal value" in December, an item on the Lee Jae-myung government's policy agenda reported to the president in August, according to reports on Oct. 18.
- Currently, the Equal Employment Opportunity Act states that "employers shall pay equal wages for work of equal value within the same business"; options reportedly under discussion include naming employment types such as fixed-term, dispatched and subcontracted work as prohibited grounds of discrimination in Article 6 of the Labor Standards Act and adding a provision mandating equal pay for the same job. A ministry official was quoted as saying "we will keep various options open, including a new provision."
- The ministry plans to analyze wage data by job, position and tenure using national statistics to set criteria for judging equal work, and the shift from seniority-based to job-based pay, unions' concern about wage cuts and expanded bargaining beyond individual companies are cited as issues, reports said.

## What we left out of the summary

- "Socialist wages" and "doesn't care about productivity" are the poster's assessments. The roadmap's details and whether the law will be amended have not yet been announced.
