This post is based on the original caption; the video content was not reviewed. The original was posted in July 2026, but the remark it covers was made on **March 1, 2025**. The original caption cites TV Chosun as its source.

## What reports confirm

- At a rally calling for President Yoon Suk Yeol's removal from office on March 1, 2025, then-Democratic Party leader Lee Jae-myung reportedly said, "If the night of insurrection on Dec. 3 had continued, I would probably have become crab food somewhere deep in the sea on the way to Yeonpyeong Island." It was reportedly said in the context of emphasizing the danger to his safety, assuming he had been arrested during the emergency martial law.
- People Power Party Rep. Bae Jun-young (배준영), whose constituency includes Yeonpyeong Island, reportedly demanded an apology to residents and to Coast Guard and Marine Corps personnel, calling it "a remark disparaging Yeonpyeong Island as a blind spot for public safety and security" and "a remark that disregards Yeonpyeong residents, whose main livelihood is crab fishing."
- Rep. Kim Eun-hye (김은혜) reportedly criticized it, saying, "On a day to honor noble souls … how dare he drag in the nation's sorrowful sea?"

## What we left out of the summary

We could not separately confirm from the reports we found this time the reactions of Yeonpyeong residents and Marine Corps personnel relayed by the original. We also did not check whether Lee's side offered an explanation or apology after the remark.
