This post is based on the original caption. Related post (may be available only in Korean): [JCS interim probe into the DMZ mine accident](/post/2026-09-28-pagememo-jcs-interim-result).

## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- It criticizes the Lee Jae-myung administration for hiding the move to South Korea of North Korean POWs held in Ukraine, then accusing Ukraine of breaking a nondisclosure agreement when President Zelensky revealed it in a UN speech.
- It speculates that the Yoon Suk Yeol (윤석열) administration's friendly stance toward Ukraine likely laid the groundwork for the POWs' transfer, and that Ukraine made the announcement because the government was disclosing neither the mine issue nor the North Korean POW issue.
- It mentions the Moon Jae-in (문재인) administration's 2019 forced repatriation of defector fishermen, conveys the anxiety and desperation of North Korean defectors, and writes that it hopes the POWs will safely find freedom.

## What reports confirm

- In a speech to the UN General Assembly on Sept. 23 (local time), President Zelensky reportedly said that two North Korean soldiers captured by Ukrainian forces had recently been sent to South Korea.
- The South Korean government's position is reportedly that it had agreed with Ukraine to proceed without disclosure, considering the repercussions for inter-Korean and Korea–Russia relations and the personal safety of the POWs and their families; President Lee reportedly wrote on X (formerly Twitter) on Sept. 24 that "nondisclosure was right in every respect, and that is what we agreed," and "I don't know what the circumstances were, but it is very regrettable."
- The Ukrainian side reportedly countered that only the details were meant to be kept confidential, with the two sides reportedly differing over the scope of disclosure.

## What we left out of the summary

- The claim in the original and its image that "the forcibly repatriated fishermen were tortured for some 50 days and then beheaded" quotes the headline of a 2023 article by one outlet; it has never been reported as a fact confirmed by the government or a credible institution, so we did not treat it as fact. The original's speculation about why Ukraine made the disclosure was also not written as fact.
