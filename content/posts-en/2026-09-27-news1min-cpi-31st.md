The original was posted on Sept. 27, 2026, but the announcement the caption covers is the Anti-Corruption and Civil Rights Commission's (ACRC) **Feb. 10, 2026** announcement. The caption cites Hankook Ilbo (photo) and Joongang Economy News (중앙이코노미뉴스) (article) as sources.

## What reports confirm

- The ACRC reportedly announced on Feb. 10, 2026, that Korea scored 63 out of 100 on Transparency International's "2025 Corruption Perceptions Index (CPI)," ranking 31st among 182 countries. This was reportedly down one point and one spot from the 2024 survey (64 points, 30th).
- Korea reportedly ranked 22nd among the OECD's 38 members, and this was reportedly the first score decline since 2017.
- The ACRC reportedly analyzed that "a decline in the business-survey indicator due to economic uncertainty in the first half of last year was the main factor," and that "political and economic uncertainty since the December 2024 martial-law decree appears to have weighed sensitively on expert assessments and business surveys, creating downward pressure on the score."
- Denmark (89 points) reportedly ranked first, followed by Finland (88), Singapore (84), and New Zealand and Norway (81).
- In the Asia-Pacific region, after Singapore and New Zealand, Australia and Hong Kong (tied for 12th, 76 points), Japan and Bhutan (tied for 18th, 71 points), and Taiwan (tied for 24th, 68 points) reportedly ranked higher than Korea. North Korea reportedly scored 15 points, ranking 172nd.
- The ACRC reportedly said it would overhaul the anti-corruption system with a goal of entering the top 20.
