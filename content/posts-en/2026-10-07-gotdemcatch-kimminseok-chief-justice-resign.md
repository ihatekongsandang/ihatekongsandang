## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- It says Democratic Party leader Kim Min-seok (김민석) told Chief Justice Cho Hee-dae (조희대) that "if he can't handle that problem, he'd better step down," effectively demanding his resignation over the re-nomination of Supreme Court justice candidates.
- It says the Democratic Party is pressing on with attacks, raising Cho's refusal to testify at the parliamentary audit and his business expenses, and criticizes that "it can sound like 'step down if you don't re-nominate the way the presidential office and the ruling party want'" and asks whether a dispute over powers between constitutional bodies should end in "quit if you don't listen."

## What reports confirm

- Appearing on "Minti 07" on the Democratic Party's YouTube channel on Oct. 7, Kim reportedly said of Chief Justice Cho, regarding the re-nomination of Supreme Court justice candidates, that "if he is in a state where he can't handle that problem like that, it would honestly be better for him to step down," and "that is how I see it, by ordinary judgment."
- On Cho's business expenses being raised at the judiciary committee's audit the previous day, Kim reportedly said "the chief justice must have been in an awkward position, embarrassed at having to talk even about omakase," but that "the root is that he failed to handle the re-nomination issue properly."
- Chief Justice Cho's position was reportedly that re-nominating Supreme Court justice candidates has no basis in the Constitution.

## What we left out of the summary

- **"Kim Min-seok removes Cho Hee-dae," "Resign if you won't listen to Lee Jae-myung," "Otherwise I'll do it my way"**: No report says he said these things. The reported remark is that it would be better for Cho to step down "if he can't handle" the re-nomination issue.
- "It can sound like 'quit if you don't follow the presidential office'" and "removal specialist" are the poster's interpretations and assessments.
