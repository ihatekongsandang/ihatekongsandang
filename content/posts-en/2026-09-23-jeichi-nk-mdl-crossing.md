This post is based on the original thumbnail text; the caption was empty, so the video content was not reviewed. The thumbnail image appears to be a composited or generated illustration rather than an actual scene photo.

## What reports confirm

- Around 10:50 a.m. on Sept. 21, 2026, two blasts presumed to be mines reportedly occurred in the DMZ on the western front under the Army 25th Division's jurisdiction in Paju, Gyeonggi Province, injuring three officers.
- MBC reported that, before the blast that same day, North Korean troops briefly crossed the Military Demarcation Line on the **eastern front** and then withdrew, and that the South Korean military responded according to operational procedure.
- According to the same report, in the area where the blast occurred and where a North Korean minefield is suspected — the **western front** — North Korean troops did not cross the Military Demarcation Line, and there was reportedly no unusual activity.

## What we left out of the summary

The phrase "it's currently a quasi-wartime situation" is the original poster's own claim; there is no report that the government or military declared any state of quasi-war. A link between the eastern-front crossing and the western-front blast is also not confirmed by reports.
