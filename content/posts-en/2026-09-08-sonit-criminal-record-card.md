## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- Saying that there are people with criminal records among the president, ministers and lawmakers, that the prosecution criminals hate is gone, and that most ordinary citizens have no criminal record, it asks, "Where is this country heading?" It is headed "'Daehan People's Republic' (대한인민공화국)."

## What reports confirm

- It was reported that during the 2022 presidential election, four criminal records of President Lee Jae-myung were disclosed — false accusation and impersonating a public official (a 1.5-million-won fine in 2003), drunk driving (a 1.5-million-won fine in 2004), damaging public property and aggravated obstruction of official duties (a 500,000-won fine finalized in 2011), and a violation of the Public Official Election Act — and that three of them were listed in his campaign booklet under the disclosure standard (fines of 1 million won or more).
- An amendment to the Criminal Procedure Act abolishing prosecutors' supplementary investigation authority passed the National Assembly on July 31, and the Prosecutors' Office is scheduled to be abolished on Oct. 2 and split into the Public Prosecution Agency (공소청) and the Serious Crimes Investigation Agency (중대범죄수사청). On Sept. 8, when the original was posted, the Prosecutors' Office had not yet been abolished.
