## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- It says that over Hyundai Group's $450 million remittance to North Korea ahead of the 2000 inter-Korean summit, then–presidential chief of staff Park Jie-won (박지원) was tried, with the Supreme Court in 2006 upholding a conviction on remittance-related charges while finding Park not guilty of receiving 15 billion won in Hyundai slush funds.
- On the Moon Jae-in government's appointment of Park as director of the National Intelligence Service in 2020, it presents both the view questioning fitness for a national security post and the view valuing Park's experience negotiating with the North, concluding that "the question of qualifications for a national security chief remains weighty."

## What reports confirm

- On Sept. 28, 2006, the Supreme Court dismissed the prosecution's appeal in the case of former minister Park, indicted over Hyundai slush funds and the remittance to North Korea, upholding the lower court ruling that found Park not guilty of receiving 15 billion won in Hyundai slush funds (bribery under the Act on the Aggravated Punishment of Specific Crimes) and sentenced Park to three years in prison on the remittance-related charges, according to reports. Park had been detained by the remittance special counsel in 2003 and sentenced to 12 years at the first and second trials, it was reported.
- According to reports at the time of the July 2020 nomination as NIS director, Park received a special pardon after the sentence in the remittance case was finalized and was elected to the National Assembly the following year; the then-opposition United Future Party said it would focus questioning at the confirmation hearing on the remittance case and Park's views on North Korea.

## What we left out of the summary

- The original's "violation of the Foreign Exchange Transactions Act" and the $450 million remittance figure were not checked against the ruling or contemporary reports for this post. Park's fitness as NIS director is a matter of differing assessments, and the original also presented both views.
