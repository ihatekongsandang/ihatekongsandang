## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- Saying "Found the one that had disappeared" and "This must never be deleted," it asks people to repost. The quoted video begins with a screen of a Dec. 3, 2024, CNN article and the caption "For North Korea to launch a surprise attack on South Korea."

## What reports confirm

- CNN reported on Dec. 3, 2024 (U.S. Eastern Time), that a Chinese national living illegally in California had been arrested by the FBI on charges of helping the North Korean regime prepare a surprise attack on South Korea, and the article remains publicly available on CNN's website as of September 2026.
- According to the U.S. Justice Department, the man entered the United States on a student visa in 2012 and overstayed, and on the instructions of North Korean officials shipped at least three containers holding firearms and other items from the Port of Long Beach to North Korea via China in 2023, receiving about $2 million. He told the FBI that North Korea wanted weapons, U.S. military uniforms and other items for a surprise attack on South Korea.
- He pleaded guilty in June 2025 and was sentenced to eight years in prison in August of that year.

## What we left out of the summary

- Contrary to the original's word "disappeared," we found no report that the CNN article was deleted and then restored. We found no basis in the reports for treating the fact that the article was published on the same day as the Dec. 3 declaration of emergency martial law as a link between the two events.
