This post is based on the original caption and video (about 5 seconds, a title over a photo of Minister Chung). The original was posted on Sept. 18, 2026. We used a card made by this site instead of the original image.

## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- It says Unification Minister Chung Dong-young (정동영) announced that the government would not file damages suits over the South Korean facilities at the Kaesong Industrial Complex and Mt. Kumgang demolished by North Korea, noting the announcement came after a court ordered North Korea to pay 44.6 billion won over the demolition of the inter-Korean liaison office.
- It says Chung, while respecting the ruling, cited that compulsory enforcement is effectively impossible, so a suit would be ineffective.

## What reports confirm

- On Sept. 16, 2026, the Seoul Central District Court's 46th Civil Division ruled for the government in its damages suit against North Korea over the demolition of the inter-Korean joint liaison office, granting the full claim of 44,626,410,000 won, according to reports. It was the first damages suit the government filed against North Korean authorities; the Unification Ministry filed it in June 2023, and reports noted that actual enforcement would be difficult.
- At the National Assembly's foreign affairs and unification committee on Sept. 17, Minister Chung reportedly said that in October 2025 the ministry decided not to sue over North Korea's unilateral demolition of South Korean facilities at Mt. Kumgang and Kaesong because compulsory enforcement is effectively impossible, making a suit ineffective. On the liaison office ruling, Chung reportedly said "we respect the judiciary's judgment."

## What we left out of the summary

- The original's "despite the ruling" can read as if the suit was abandoned after the ruling, but according to reports, the decision not to file suits over Mt. Kumgang and Kaesong was made in October 2025 and is a separate matter from the liaison office case.
