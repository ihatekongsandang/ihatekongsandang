## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- It relays that Cho Myung-hyun, who blew the whistle on suspicions over the use of Gyeonggi Province corporate cards, spoke out again after five years, saying he decided to report after seeing public funds used for private purposes and even the people around him growing used to it.
- It says Cho confided that although he feared the fallout after his tip-off, he did not stay silent because "if I had pretended not to know all my life, I would have felt guilty until the day I died," and that he and his family went through great emotional hardship.
- It argues that what is needed is fact-finding according to law and principle, and that the halted trial involving President Lee Jae-myung should also resume in accordance with law and procedure.

## What reports confirm

- Prosecutors reportedly indicted President Lee in November 2024 on charges of misappropriating budget funds, including Gyeonggi Province corporate cards (occupational breach of trust), and gave first lady Kim Hye-kyung (김혜경) a suspended indictment. This trial was reportedly suspended, along with his other criminal trials, after he took office as president in June 2025.
- Kim Hye-kyung was reportedly fined 1.5 million won at both the first-instance and appellate trials in a Public Official Election Act case over paying 104,000 won for meals with a Gyeonggi Province corporate card in 2021.
- In a damages suit Cho Myung-hyun filed against Gyeonggi Province and others, the Suwon District Court in January 2026 reportedly ruled partly in the plaintiff's favor, ordering the province and others to pay 20 million won in compensation, and the province reportedly appealed.
