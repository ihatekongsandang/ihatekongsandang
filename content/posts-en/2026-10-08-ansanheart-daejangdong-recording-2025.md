This post is based on the original caption and the reel's thumbnail and first frame. The original thumbnail reads "Urgent breaking news / New Daejang-dong evidence / Phone recording," but the recording in the video was confirmed to have been released about 11 months earlier, in November 2025, so we did not use the original image.

## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- Asking "Has new evidence emerged in a phone recording of the Daejang-dong group?", it presents parts of what it says is a call between Y, former planning director of Seongnam Development Corporation, and lawyer N ("while giving the Daejang-dong project rights…", "will get (them) out during the president's term").
- The caption adds that the key questions are when the call took place, its context, whether anything was carried out and whether it matches other evidence, and that "claims in a recording alone do not prove a crime," so fact-checking must come first.

## What reports confirm

- B, who runs a YouTube channel and is a former Democratic Party dues-paying member, held a press conference at the National Assembly on Nov. 12, 2025 with People Power Party lawmaker Kim Eun-hye (김은혜) and released part of a recording presumed to be a call between N and Y, both defendants in the Daejang-dong case, it was reported. B reportedly described the call as taking place in "spring 2023" and did not disclose how it was obtained, citing protection of the source.
- In the released recording, Y reportedly said, in effect, that Lee Jae-myung and three other people (private individuals, so we do not name them) "all colluded," and N reportedly said "[K, a Daejang-dong defendant] told a lot of people (K) would only serve three years" and "it seems there was an understanding like 'just hold out for three years, we'll get you out during the president's term.'" Y reportedly said, "It was all planned long before I was arrested."
- B reportedly argued the recording shows that some Democrats' claim of collusion between defendant Y and prosecutors is false. According to reports, Y and N were sentenced at first instance in the Daejang-dong case to 8 and 4 years in prison, respectively.

## What we left out of the summary

- **"New evidence" and "urgent breaking news"**: The recording in the video was released on Nov. 12, 2025; we found no report of new material emerging in October 2026.
- "It seems there was an understanding" and similar lines are speculation in a conversation between defendants, and the recording alone does not prove the facts (as the original caption also notes). We did not find responses from those named in the recording in the reports we checked.
