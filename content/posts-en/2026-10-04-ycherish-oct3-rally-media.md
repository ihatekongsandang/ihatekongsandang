A [Threads post by @idolovekorea](https://www.threads.com/@idolovekorea/post/DeDZKkukiG_) on the same rally (a card reading "200,000 at Gwanghwamun on National Foundation Day"), a [Threads post by @5nly__u](https://www.threads.com/@5nly__u/post/DeEJFMrEVj-) sharing the same card with "it's not on the news," and a [Threads post by @yujin.park99](https://www.threads.com/@yujin.park99/post/DeDPg9rEnZC) posting aerial video of the rally with "not a single article — a sorry reality," and a [Threads post by @asiaqqq1023](https://www.threads.com/@asiaqqq1023/post/DeCmCOKjzEX) sharing a card titled "The real National Foundation Day scenes not shown on the news," and a [Threads post by @jwapaloma](https://www.threads.com/@jwapaloma/post/DeDnpr8D8EK) saying "however much the broadcast cameras look away, they can't hide this scale" are also noted here instead of in separate posts. This post is based on the original text and first image; the original has six images, of which only the first was reviewed. The first image combines a photo of President Lee Jae-myung's face with an aerial photo of a rally around Gwanghwamun, so we used a card made by this site instead of the original image. Reported attendance and on-site photos are covered separately in [Tens of thousands gather around Gwanghwamun and Sungnyemun](/post/2026-10-03-oct3-gwanghwamun-rally-scale). Posts urging people to attend this rally in advance were not published; this post is based on reports after the rally took place.

## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- It writes: "People keep raising their voices on social media, so why is the media so quiet?"
- The first image reads "Crowds gathered at the Lee Jae-myung impeachment rally, but the media is silent," over an aerial photo of a rally around Gwanghwamun.

## What reports confirm

- The Liberty Unification Party (자유통일당), centered on Pastor Jeon Kwang-hoon (전광훈) of Sarang Jeil Church, reportedly held the "Oct. 3 Gwanghwamun National Rally for the Resignation of the Lee Jae-myung Government" on Sejong-daero from 1 p.m. on Oct. 3, with about 28,000 people reportedly attending. Participants reportedly held signs reading "Lee Jae-myung, step down" and "Lee Jae-myung, stand trial" and marched to Singyo intersection from 4 p.m.
- The People Power Party reportedly held a "National Action Rally to Judge the Lee Jae-myung Government" in front of Sungnyemun from 3 p.m. the same day, reportedly its first large-scale street rally in downtown Seoul in a year.
- These rallies were reported in afternoon articles on Oct. 3 by Segye Ilbo (세계일보), Herald Economy (헤럴드경제), Digital Times (디지털타임스), Hankook Ilbo and others, some based on reports by Yonhap and News1.

## What we left out of the summary

- **"The media is silent"**: As shown above, several outlets reported the rallies' taking place, attendance and slogans on the day. This post does not judge whether the volume or prominence of coverage was appropriate.
- The original's first image calls it a "Lee Jae-myung impeachment rally," but the reported name of the Liberty Unification Party rally is the "Gwanghwamun National Rally for the Resignation of the Lee Jae-myung Government."
- We could not confirm whether the aerial photo in the first image was taken at the Oct. 3 rally.
- Reported attendance at the People Power Party rally at Sungnyemun varied (9,000 per Segye Ilbo, Herald Economy and others; about 100 per Hankook Ilbo).
- Slogans about election fraud chanted at the rally are unverified claims and were left out of the summary.
- **"200,000 at Gwanghwamun on National Foundation Day"** (@idolovekorea's card): The reports we checked put attendance at the Liberty Unification Party's Gwanghwamun rally at about 28,000, and we found no report of "200,000" even including the People Power Party rally at Sungnyemun. We also could not confirm whether the three aerial photos of Gwanghwamun and Sejong-daero used in the card were taken on Oct. 3.
