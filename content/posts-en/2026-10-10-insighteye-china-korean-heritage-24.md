## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- Citing a Korea Heritage Service research report, it says China's three northeastern provinces designated 24 ethnic Korean (Joseonjok) traditions as national intangible cultural heritage — 19 in Jilin, 4 in Liaoning and 1 in Heilongjiang — starting with farmers' music dance and seesaw and swing in 2006, adding arirang and pansori, and haegeum music and Baekjung in 2021. It says 96 transmitters are active in the Yanbian Korean Autonomous Prefecture, and that the report flagged a local exhibition linking arirang's origin to mainland China as potentially distorting.

## What reports confirm

- According to the report "Comprehensive Study on Responding to China's Cultural Project on Korean Traditions," which the office of Democratic Party Rep. Kim Yoon-deok (김윤덕) of the National Assembly culture committee obtained from the Korea Heritage Service, 24 traditions sharing roots with Korean culture and carried on in China's ethnic Korean communities were found designated as Chinese national intangible cultural heritage (19 in Jilin, 4 in Liaoning, 1 in Heilongjiang), it was reported on Oct. 8. They reportedly include farmers' music dance, seesaw and swing, crane dance, janggu dance, traditional weddings, arirang, gayageum music, pansori, ssireum, Chuseok, kimchi-making and haegeum music.
- The 24 are not new designations but cumulative cases from 2006 to 2021, and as of August 2025, 96 of the national, provincial and prefectural transmitters in Yanbian were actually active, according to reports. The full 213-page report has reportedly not been released.
- Reports noted that China's national designations are not a policy aimed solely at ethnic Koreans; China's National Ethnic Affairs Commission says the traditions of all 55 ethnic minorities are on the national list. China's Intangible Cultural Heritage Law and its Law on Promoting Ethnic Unity and Progress, in force since 2026, reportedly define each group's traditions as part of "Chinese culture."

## What we left out of the summary
