## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- It asks whether children are being brainwashed by exposure to "commie newspapers and books."
- It claims that while bookstores sell books about North Korea and Kim Jong Un (김정은), some books on the founding of South Korea or the Korean War became "history distortion" controversy books and were "banned from publication," and writes that this means there are many spies in South Korea with strong influence.

## What reports confirm

- In August 2025, the Ministry of National Defense reportedly decided to recall and discard *Stories of the Korean War Told by Grandpa* (published January 2024), which it had selected for its barracks library for troops and distributed in about 10,000 copies, citing reasons such as glorifying the Syngman Rhee (이승만) government's land reform and insufficient historical verification of the content of President Syngman Rhee's prayer during the Nakdong River defense line period in August 1950.
- People Power Party Rep. Han Ki-ho (한기호) and the author, a retired colonel, among others, reportedly objected, saying, "Denying historical facts because the administration has changed is a distortion of history" and "It is effectively a censorship measure."
- The reported measure removed the book from the military's barracks library list and recalled the distributed copies; it did not ban the book's publication or its sale in ordinary bookstores.

## What we left out of the summary

- The part saying "there are many spies in South Korea" is the poster's assessment, and neither the original nor reports provide a basis connecting it to this matter. We could not confirm whether the original refers to books other than this one.
