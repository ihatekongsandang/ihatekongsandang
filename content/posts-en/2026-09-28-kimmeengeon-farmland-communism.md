This post is based on the original caption and video thumbnail; the full video was not reviewed. The original is a post from Rep. Kim Min-jeon (김민전)'s own account, and the date of the session where the questioning took place could not be confirmed. Related post (may be available only in Korean): [reel criticizing the full farmland survey](/post/2026-09-27-geonwoo-farmland-survey).

## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- Isn't it a matter of communist thinking for the government to decide how an individual's land is used?

## What reports confirm

- The presidential office reportedly countered on Sept. 22 that regarding the full farmland survey, "there's also talk calling it communism, which is really absurd," and dismissed claims that farmland would be sold off cheaply as "close to excessive agitation." Earlier, People Power Party leader Jang Dong-hyuk (장동혁) reportedly criticized it, asking, "Do you want to head toward communism?"
- President Lee Jae-myung reportedly said on Sept. 24 that the full farmland survey was a constitutional duty to uphold the "farmland-to-the-tiller" principle, and that elderly farmers' land and inherited farmland were not targets for disposal.

## What we left out of the summary

We could not find media reports specifically covering Rep. Kim Min-jeon's questioning remarks themselves. The characterization "communist thinking" is the opposition lawmaker's own political claim.
