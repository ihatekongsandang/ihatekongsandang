This post is based on the original caption; the video content was not reviewed. The events covered took place in **September 2023**.

## What reports confirm

- In September 2023, the Seoul Central District Prosecutors' Office reportedly requested an arrest warrant for then-Democratic Party leader Lee Jae-myung on charges of preferential treatment in the Baekhyeon-dong development (breach of trust), Ssangbangwool's remittances to North Korea (third-party bribery) and inducing perjury, and included in the 142-page request the argument that "a prison sentence of 11 years or more and 36 years and 6 months or less, or life imprisonment" should be imposed.
- Reports say the National Assembly passed the motion consenting to his arrest, but Seoul Central District Court senior judge Yoo Chang-hoon (유창훈), in charge of warrants, denied the warrant on Sept. 27, 2023.
- As grounds for the denial, he reportedly said, "In the cases of inducing perjury and the Baekhyeon-dong development project, it is difficult to see a risk of destroying evidence in light of the human and physical evidence secured so far," and judged that the Baekhyeon-dong and North Korea remittance charges were open to dispute.

## What we left out of the summary

"36 years and 6 months or life in prison" was the prosecution's argument at the warrant-request stage, not a court ruling. Denial of a warrant is not a judgment of guilt or innocence, and the trial outcomes of those cases are not covered in this post.
