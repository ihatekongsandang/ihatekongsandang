This post is based on the original caption; the video content was not reviewed. The original was posted on July 1, 2026, but the remarks it covers were made at a press meeting in Shanghai during his China visit on **Jan. 7, 2026**. The caption cites SBS News as the source of the video.

## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- In light of the history of repeated North Korean armed provocations — the invasion that started the Korean War, the sinking of the Cheonan, the shelling of Yeonpyeong Island and the wooden-box mine provocation — the expression "we carried out military attacks on North Korea for a long time" is inappropriate.

## What reports confirm

- At a luncheon press meeting in Shanghai on Jan. 7, 2026, wrapping up a four-day, three-night state visit to China, President Lee Jae-myung reportedly said, "(We) carried out military attacks on North Korea for quite a long time," and "North Korea must have been extremely anxious."
- He then reportedly said, in effect, that to have a dialogue one must understand the other side's position, asked, "Will you call it pro-North to side with North Korea?", and added, "This is the cold reality."
- At the same meeting, he also reportedly used expressions such as "Dialogue is not easy because it provokes enormous wariness and hostility" and "karma accumulated over quite a long time."
- The remarks reportedly sparked criticism and controversy over what critics called a biased view of North Korea.

## What we left out of the summary

The reports we found this time did not specify which acts the president meant by "military attacks."
