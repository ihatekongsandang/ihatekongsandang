This post is based on the original caption; the video content was not reviewed. The events covered took place in **2018–2020**. The daughter of the late Lee Jae-sun (이재선) is a private individual, so her name is not given.

## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- The public should examine for themselves what sister-in-law Park In-bok (박인복) pleaded in her 2018 press conference and 2019 court testimony, along with the recordings and records from the time, and judge for themselves; the poster hopes the family's honor will be restored. (The original also notes that "this does not mean all of the court testimony has been established as fact.")

## What reports confirm

- During the June 2018 local elections, Park In-bok reportedly held a press conference with Kim Young-hwan (김영환), the Bareunmirae Party's candidate for Gyeonggi governor, and said there was a recording of Kim Hye-kyung (김혜경) talking on the phone with a niece or nephew about matters related to Lee Jae-sun's forced hospitalization.
- At the ninth hearing of the trial on charges including the "forced hospitalization of his older brother" on March 11, 2019, Park In-bok and her daughter reportedly appeared as prosecution witnesses and, at their request, did not face the defendant in the courtroom.
- It was reported that then-Gyeonggi Governor Lee Jae-myung was indicted on charges of ordering his older brother's forced hospitalization as mayor of Seongnam (abuse of authority) and of disseminating false information in a TV debate, that he was acquitted of abuse of authority at both the first and second instances, and that on the false-information charge he was acquitted at first instance and found guilty at second instance (a 3-million-won fine), before the Supreme Court in July 2020 reversed and remanded the case with a view to acquittal and he was acquitted at the retrial on remand on Oct. 16 of that year.

## What we left out of the summary

The parties' claims about the content of the recording and the family conflict contradict one another, and this post does not judge whose claims are true.
