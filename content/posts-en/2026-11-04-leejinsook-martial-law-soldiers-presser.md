This post is based on the original caption and the press conference video (about 8 minutes, 12 frames); we did not transcribe the full remarks. An [Instagram reel by @jugwan_ilbo](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfT6s46iYjy/) (Nov. 4) that introduced the same press conference as "Exposing the Lee Jae-myung regime — shocking revelation," and an [Instagram reel by @wikipost_mag](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfUjVLUuRxo/) (Nov. 4) whose caption carries the full statement Y read at the same press conference, are noted here instead of in separate posts. The statement's criticisms of the government are the speaker's opinions, so we did not carry them into the summary.

## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- Under the title "A country that tramples on soldiers' honor," it says free South Korea dies in a country where soldiers cannot feel pride and are stripped of their honor.
- It asks people to share the video, saying that even if the media do not report much on the press conference, more people will learn the truth if it is shared on social media.

## What reports confirm

- At a press conference at the National Assembly on Nov. 4 with Lt. Col. Y, who served in the Special Warfare Command during the Dec. 3 martial law, People Power Party lawmaker Lee Jin-sook (이진숙) said "many service members were sent to so-called insurrection trials or given heavy punishment such as dismissal, discharge, demotion or suspension for following their superiors' orders during the Dec. 3 martial law," according to reports (Edaily).
- Lee said Y had recently left the military; Y commanded the 52nd Special Forces Battalion of the 9th Special Forces Brigade during the martial law and led troops to the Yeoronjosa-kkot building, it was reported. Y reportedly said "many generals and field-grade officers have been disciplined and trials are under way" and that "most serving soldiers neither accept nor understand the government's measures."

## What we left out of the summary

- Lee's and Y's assessments (such as that "national defense could collapse because of this government") are the speakers' opinions. The specific scale of investigations and discipline of soldiers involved in the martial law, and the defense ministry's position, did not appear in the reports we checked, so we did not state them.
