This post is based on the original caption; the video content was not reviewed. The original was posted on July 28, 2026, and the first-instance ruling came afterward, on Sept. 11.

## What reports confirm

- Reports say that on July 24, 2026, former President Yoon Suk Yeol (윤석열) made his final statement at the closing hearing of his trial on charges including "harboring a criminal" for appointing former Defense Minister Lee Jong-sup (이종섭), then a suspect in the Cpl. Chae case, as ambassador to Australia and letting him leave the country, and that the special counsel sought five years in prison.
- Yoon reportedly said to the special counsel, "If you indict things like this recklessly, what will become of the country?" and "You think you'll be safe?", raising the question of accountability for baseless investigations and indictments.
- Finally, he reportedly asked, in effect, that the responsibility of the other co-defendants be lessened, saying, "If the court takes a different view from mine, this is something I pursued under the president's constitutional diplomatic powers, so all responsibility rests with me."
- On Sept. 11, Criminal Division 22 of the Seoul Central District Court reportedly acquitted former President Yoon of harboring a criminal, saying "there is some room to view it as part of the exercise of personnel authority as head of state" and that it was difficult to conclude there was an intent to help him flee.

## What we left out of the summary

We could not confirm from the reports we found this time whether the special counsel appealed the first-instance acquittal.
