## What reports confirm

- A petition by Gyeonggi residents demanding the resignation of Gyeonggi Governor Choo Mi-ae (추미애) reportedly cited the declaration of a fiscal emergency, austerity budgeting and cuts to livelihood budgets such as postpartum care subsidies, and the petitioners reportedly criticized the province for spending provincial funds on the governor's official residence and official car even amid austerity.
- When the petition passed the threshold requiring a response from the governor, Gyeonggi Province reportedly answered with a written briefing and marked it "answered," and the petition reportedly closed with 30,775 signatures.
- In the written briefing, the province reportedly said it "deeply regrets the distortion of facts as if the 9th popularly elected provincial administration had deliberately cut livelihood budgets that had been duly earmarked," and asked for understanding of its fiscal situation.
- At a real estate conference held the day the petition closed, Governor Choo reportedly likened herself to "a daughter-in-law who married into Gyeonggi Province," saying the coffers were empty, and said, "I, Choo Mi-ae, won't be driven out."
