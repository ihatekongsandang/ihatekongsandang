This post is based on the original caption ("Only Lee Jae-myung's trial") and the video thumbnail; the full video was not reviewed. The thumbnail carries the captions "Lee Jae-myung was already indicted, so why has the trial stopped?" and "Why does it remain in a suspended state?"

Rep. Yoon also posted [another reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdQOdrhSeFF/) of the same questioning with the caption "The grounds for halting Lee Jae-myung's trial that even a 28-year judge can't answer," which is noted here instead of in a separate post.

## What reports confirm

- At the Sept. 14, 2026, confirmation hearing for Supreme Court justice nominee Kim Sung-soo (김성수), People Power Party Rep. Yoon Yong-geun (윤용근) reportedly asked, "Is there any provision in the current Constitution and laws stating that a criminal trial already indicted and under way before the president took office must be halted?" and "Do you, nominee, also think the criminal trial of defendant Lee Jae-myung should resume right away?"
- When nominee Kim answered, "The trial panel suspended it after completing its legal review," Rep. Yoon reportedly asked back, "Is the judiciary violating the Constitution and laws by halting the trial of defendant Lee Jae-myung?"
- Nominee Kim reportedly answered, "For a specific case, the trial panel reviews it and proceeds with the procedures in consideration of various circumstances, so it is difficult for me to say whether it was appropriate."
- At the same hearing, the ruling party reportedly took issue with Chief Justice Cho Hee-dae (조희대)'s recommendation of justices and with the speed of the ruling to reverse and remand in the Public Official Election Act case during the last presidential election, leading to clashes between the parties.
