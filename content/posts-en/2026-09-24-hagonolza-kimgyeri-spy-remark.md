This post is based on the original caption and the first image. The original was posted on Sept. 24, 2026, but the content it covers is a **June 2026** report.

## What reports confirm

- On June 12, 2026, Criminal Division 36 of the Seoul Central District Court reportedly sentenced former President Yoon Suk Yeol (윤석열) to 30 years in prison in the so-called "Pyongyang drone allegations" case, in which he was indicted on charges of general aiding of the enemy (일반이적) and abuse of authority obstructing the exercise of rights.
- His defense lawyer Kim Kye-ri (김계리) reportedly wrote on Facebook on June 13, "The reason I cried was not that the president received a 30-year sentence," and claimed that while analyzing cases such as the KCTU spy-directive case in preparing the defense, "I felt firsthand the spy problem deeply rooted in our society, and it was frightening to the point of giving me chills."
- Kim reportedly claimed the trial should have been broadcast and made public, and criticized security policy, citing the Moon Jae-in administration's transfer of the NIS's anti-communist investigative authority and the Lee Jae-myung administration's push to reorganize the Defense Counterintelligence Command and abolish the Drone Operations Command.

## What we left out of the summary

"Had the trial been public, a guilty verdict could not have been handed down" is the defense lawyer's claim. We could not confirm the status of the appeal in this case from the reports we found this time.
