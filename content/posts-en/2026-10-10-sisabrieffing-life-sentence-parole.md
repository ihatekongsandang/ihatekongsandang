This post is based on the original caption, and we used a card made by this site instead of the original image.

## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- Citing Weekly Chosun, it says 13 life-sentence prisoners received parole this year, already exceeding the 9 for all of 2025; that the justice ministry explained it is expanding parole because of overcrowding at 125% of capacity; and that Rep. Joo Jin-woo argued lifers should be exempt from parole. It asks, "Should easing prison overcrowding come first, or limiting parole for violent criminals?"

## What reports confirm

- At the National Assembly Legislation and Judiciary Committee's audit of the justice ministry on Oct. 8, People Power Party Rep. Joo Jin-woo (주진우) said, citing data Joo presented, that 13 life-sentence prisoners were paroled this year, compared with 9 in 2025 and 1 in 2024, and asked, "What do you intend to do by releasing people who committed rape-murder and robbery-murder?", demanding an exception for lifers, it was reported.
- Acting Justice Minister Lee Jin-su (이진수) reportedly replied that "under the law, even those sentenced to life imprisonment may be paroled after serving 20 years or more," and that "it is clearly true that we are expanding parole in a situation where prison overcrowding has reached 125%," adding that the parole review committee carefully examines the circumstances of the case, the sentence and the risk of reoffending.

## What we left out of the summary

- We could not directly check the Weekly Chosun report the original cites; the figures were checked against reports on the audit questioning, such as Sisa Journal. Some reports say all 13 were murderers, but this post did not check the individual cases.
