This post is based on the original caption and thumbnail (one video frame); the full video was not reviewed. Profanity and derogatory language at the start of the original caption are not reproduced. A post from just after the first-instance ruling in the same case (may be available only in Korean): [Lawyer Kim Gye-ri: "So many spies it gives me chills"](/post/2026-09-24-hagonolza-kimgyeri-spy-remark).

## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- After the poster's opinion (with derogatory language) that those pushing U.S. troop withdrawal, wartime operational control transfer and the dismantling of the counterintelligence command are the ones guilty of benefiting the enemy, it relays the following as remarks former Defense Minister Kim Yong-hyun (김용현) made in a final statement at the appeal hearing on Oct. 16, 2026.
- Saying security experts warn that South Korea could become a second killing field if it turned communist, it describes the 1975 massacres under Pol Pot in Cambodia and cites, "regardless of its credibility," a remark attributed to Kim Jong-il by former Workers' Party secretary Hwang Jang-yop (that if the peninsula were unified under communism, 10 million of the South's 50 million people would be executed, and so on), saying no one should stand by on national security.

## What reports confirm

- At the appeal closing hearing on Oct. 16, 2026, before the Seoul High Court's 1st Criminal Division, the special counsel team led by Cho Eun-suk sought 30 years in prison each for former President Yoon Suk Yeol (윤석열) and Kim on charges of benefiting the enemy, with the ruling set for Dec. 4, according to reports. They are accused of ordering the drone operations command around October 2024 to fly drones into Pyongyang to create grounds for declaring martial law; Kim had been sentenced at first instance to 30 years, heavier than the 25 years prosecutors sought, it was reported.
- In the final statement, Kim reportedly said "the special counsel's claim that the drone operation raised the risk of military clashes and caused loss of life and property is fiction" and "rather, the drone operation deterred North Korea's trash balloon provocations and secured safety," pleading not guilty. Yoon reportedly called the first-instance trial "judicial terror under the guise of a trial."

## What we left out of the summary

- The "killing field" passage and the Kim Jong-il quote that the original attributes to Kim's final statement were not found in the reports we checked. The original's video frame also carries the captions "2026.10.16 benefiting-the-enemy final statement" and "The killing field is by no means someone else's affair," but we could not confirm the video's source. The Kim Jong-il remark attributed by Hwang Jang-yop is hearsay, which the original itself qualifies with "regardless of its credibility." Guilt has not been finally determined, as the appeal ruling is pending.
