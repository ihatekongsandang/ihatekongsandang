An [Instagram post by @orca.of.truth](https://www.instagram.com/p/DdGpQ7JT_B8/) on Yu's Sept. 10 parliamentary questioning (posted Sept. 10, a card reading "North Korea crossed the MDL by 15 m to lay mines / Pressed on with mine work ignoring warning shots / Rep. Yu Yong-weon points to security being neutralized") is noted here instead of in a separate post (see the last section). This post is based on the reel's caption and may differ from the full video content.

## Caption (verbatim)

> "I strongly urge the government and military authorities to swiftly and transparently disclose the truth regarding North Korean troops' installation of a minefield south of the MDL and the 25th Division mine blast!"

## What reports confirm

- Rep. Yu Yong-weon (유용원) reportedly said, in effect, that the 25th Division blast site was near a minefield created by North Korea and that whoever laid the mine must be identified (Newspim, Herald Economy).
- On Sept. 21, three officers were reportedly injured in a blast presumed to be a mine during a 25th Division DMZ search operation, and who laid it was reportedly under investigation.

## Also noted here — @orca.of.truth (Sept. 10)

- The original claims: It says Rep. Yu revealed that North Korean troops crossed the MDL by as much as 15 m to lay mines and build roads on South Korean territory, seizing land, and criticizes the government for standing by even as the North presses on with intrusive construction, scoffing at warning shots.
- What reports confirm: At the National Assembly's questioning of the government on Sept. 10, Yu reportedly pointed out that in the 25th Division's guard area alone, six sites including minefields created by North Korean troops intruded about 10–15 m across the MDL (as cited by Kyeongin Ilbo on Sept. 21). At an Oct. 5 press conference, Yu reportedly argued that the government and military had played down and distorted the matter at the time with the vague phrase "some terrain changes were identified" (Seoul News Agency).
- On Oct. 5, the JCS reportedly made a final determination that the Sept. 21 blast was caused by a mine North Korea laid south of the MDL and said several presumed North Korean minefields south of the MDL intrude about 20 m on average ([related post](/post/2026-10-05-shindawit-mdl-south-minefields-20m)).
- What we left out of the summary: The reported figure is that six sites "including minefields" intruded "about 10–15 m." Of the phrase "crossed by 15 m to lay mines and build roads, seizing land," whether roads were built south of the MDL was not confirmed in reports on the Sept. 10 remarks. The line that the North "scoffed at warning shots and pressed on with construction" was also not confirmed in reports on the Sept. 10 questioning. "The government stood by and gave up even the will to defend the country" is the poster's assessment.
