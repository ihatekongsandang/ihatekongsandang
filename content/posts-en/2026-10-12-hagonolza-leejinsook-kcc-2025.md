This post is based on the original caption and its 11 images. The original reposts a card-news series from another account (@tantanroad.official), and the content concerns events around **Sept. 30, 2025**, when then–Korea Communications Commission (KCC) Chair Lee Jin-sook was removed as the KCC was abolished. We used a card made by this site instead of the original images. A post on Rep. Lee's recent remarks (may be available only in Korean): [Criticism of the film "Assassin(s)"](/post/2026-09-29-leejinsook-assassins-film-distortion).

## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- Calling it "the Lee Jae-myung government's first purge," it says KCC Chair Lee Jin-sook (이진숙) ended work on Sept. 30, saying "the rule of law in South Korea died today," and commented on the KCC's abolition and the chair's automatic removal that "it makes me think that if things don't go according to current law, they can change the law to remove a person." It argues that turning the KCC into the Korea Media and Communications Commission will go down in history as changing the law to purge the head of a government body with time left in the term.
- It says Lee criticized the three broadcasting laws for putting all 10 broadcasters that significantly shape public opinion under joint labor-management management, effectively giving the KCTU (its media union) enormous influence.

## What reports confirm

- When the act establishing the Korea Media and Communications Commission and the revised Government Organization Act were passed by the cabinet on Sept. 30, 2025, and promulgated the next day (Oct. 1), the KCC was abolished and Chair Lee was automatically removed, it was reported. On the last day leaving the office, Lee reportedly said "the rule of law in South Korea died today" and "it makes me think that if things don't go according to current law, they can change the law to remove a person," and announced a constitutional complaint. Lee's original term reportedly ran until August 2026.
- The three broadcasting laws, which expand the number of public broadcaster board members and diversify who recommends them, passed a National Assembly plenary session in August 2025, according to reports.
- Former Chair Lee has since served as a People Power Party lawmaker.

## What we left out of the summary

- "Lee Jae-myung's first purge" and "changing the law to purge" are the assessments of the poster and the original account.
- "All 10 broadcasters go under joint labor-management management" and "the KCTU gains enormous influence" were presented as then-Chair Lee's criticism; because the broadcasting laws' actual scope differs by provision (the three public broadcasters, news channels and so on), this post did not reproduce them as fact.
- The original's final call-to-action image was not reproduced.
