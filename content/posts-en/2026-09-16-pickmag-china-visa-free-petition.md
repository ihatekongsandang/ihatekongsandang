An [Instagram post by @ccp_out_korea](https://www.instagram.com/p/DdshPP0DJok/) on illegal movement exploiting Jeju's visa-free program is noted here instead of in a separate post. This post is based on the original caption; the video content was not reviewed.

## What reports confirm

- The National Assembly public-consent petition "Petition urging a ban on visa-free entry for Chinese nationals and stronger immigration screening and crime management for foreigners," made public on Aug. 31, 2026, reportedly passed 50,000 signatures and was established on Sept. 12, and was referred on Sept. 14 to the Legislation and Judiciary Committee, the standing committee in charge. The number of signatures reportedly exceeded 60,000.
- Along with a halt to visa-free entry for Chinese nationals, the petitioner reportedly called for stronger screening so that, regardless of nationality, foreigners' criminal records, international wanted status and any history of entry refusal, deportation or illegal stay are thoroughly checked at the point of entry.
- According to material the National Police Agency submitted to the National Assembly, 34,763 foreign suspects were reportedly arrested last year, with China (15,391), Vietnam (3,838) and Uzbekistan (2,145) topping the list by nationality.

## What we left out of the summary

The petition's referral to the Judiciary Committee means it has become subject to review; it does not mean abolishing the visa-free program has been decided. The number of foreign suspects is a figure that should be read alongside the size of each nationality's resident population.

## Checking the post noted here (@ccp_out_korea)

- Jeju Coast Guard reportedly said on Aug. 12 that it had caught and referred to prosecutors three people, including a recruiter (a Chinese national in their 30s) and a domestic broker (in their 50s), for organizing the illegal transfer of visa-free entrants and undocumented foreigners off Jeju by hiding them in cargo trucks on cargo ships, with another broker wanted. The recruiter was reportedly caught in Gimhae, South Gyeongsang Province, on June 10 after about 70 days of pursuit.
- The post's "3 million won per person" was not confirmed in that report. Its figures of "about 10,700 undocumented foreigners in Jeju, 85% of them Chinese" and "about 70% of foreign crime suspects being Chinese" are described only as figures cited by politicians, and we could not confirm their reference dates.
- The line that "Korean society bears the costs of crime networks that start in China" is the poster's opinion.
