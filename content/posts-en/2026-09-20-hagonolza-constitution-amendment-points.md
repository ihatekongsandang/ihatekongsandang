## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- The core of the constitutional amendment under discussion is ① putting the spirit of the April 19 Revolution, the Bu-Ma Democratic Protests and the May 18 movement in the constitutional preamble, ② strengthening the National Assembly's powers, such as limiting the president's power to veto bills, moving the Board of Audit and Inspection under the National Assembly and expanding the requirement for National Assembly consent to appointments of key public officials, and ③ specifying South Korea as a "decentralized state" and expanding local governments' powers; it should go through national consensus and sufficient scrutiny rather than serve the interests of a particular political force.

## What reports confirm

- It was reported that on July 17, 2025, President Lee Jae-myung mentioned a "people-centered constitutional amendment" and proposed putting the May 18 Democratic Movement in the constitutional preamble, strengthening basic rights and expanding local autonomy and decentralization, and that he had presented a four-year presidential term with one reelection, moving the Board of Audit and Inspection to the National Assembly and requiring National Assembly consent to appointments of heads of neutral institutions as presidential campaign pledges.
- In April 2026, six ruling and opposition parties other than the People Power Party reportedly introduced a constitutional amendment including putting the spirit of the Bu-Ma Democratic Protests and May 18 in the preamble and tightening the requirements for martial law, but the plenary vote on May 7 was reportedly declared void for lack of a quorum because the People Power Party did not take part.
- At the interpellation session in September, Prime Minister Han Sung-sook reportedly said she would actively support a "minimal amendment," and the People Power Party reportedly took issue with what it saw as an intent to amend the Constitution to allow presidential reelection.

## What we left out of the summary

We could not confirm from the reports we found this time in what specific form the three items summarized by the original would be included in the constitutional amendment now under discussion.
