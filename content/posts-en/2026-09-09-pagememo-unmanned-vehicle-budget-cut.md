This post is based on the original caption and thumbnail; the video itself was not reviewed. The original cites a Financial News report as its primary source and a Seoul Finance report for cross-checking.

## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- It says that in the government's 2027 budget bill, funding for fielding a domestic multipurpose unmanned ground vehicle was cut by about 96%, with only 350 million won (about 4%) of the 8.8 billion won requested by the Defense Acquisition Program Administration (DAPA) included. It notes that Hanwha Aerospace's Arion-SMET was selected in July and that the program itself continues.
- It contrasts this with medium-range loitering drones bought from abroad, for which 38.353 billion won of the 40.329 billion won requested was included.
- It says whether this fits the government's emphasis on manned-unmanned teaming in response to shrinking troop numbers should be examined during the National Assembly's budget review, adding that the figures are the government's proposal, not a final budget.

## What reports confirm

- According to next year's budget bill submitted by DAPA to People Power Party Rep. Yu Yong-weon (유용원) of the National Assembly Defense Committee, DAPA requested 8.8 billion won for the multipurpose unmanned vehicle, but only 350 million won was reportedly included in the government bill. Hanwha Aerospace's Arion-SMET was reportedly selected for the program in July, with a 49.6 billion won first phase followed by a planned follow-on program of about 500 billion won.
- For medium-range loitering drones, purchased from abroad, 38.353 billion won of the 40.329 billion won DAPA requested was reportedly allocated.
- A DAPA official reportedly said, "We requested from the government only the budget that is truly needed next year," explaining that the allocation reflected the program's actual schedule and feasibility of execution.

## What we left out of the summary

- Reports put the share included at "3%" or described a "97% cut," while the original says "about 4%" and "96%" (350 million ÷ 8.8 billion ≈ 4.0%). The amounts are the same.
- "Lee Jae-myung government" in the thumbnail is the poster's wording; reports described it as the gap between DAPA's request and the government bill. The figures are the government's proposal before National Assembly review.
