## The original post (full text, translated)

> There's money to give North Korea 160 expensive medical devices, but money to give the soldiers attacked by North Korea ???

## What reports confirm

- The Ministry of Unification is reportedly pushing to provide **1,431 items of 166 types** of medical equipment (worth about $6.75 million, or 9.3 billion won) to Kangdong County Hospital in Pyongyang. The items reportedly include X-ray machines, stethoscopes, blood pressure monitors, operating tables, defibrillators and ventilators (The Financial News).
- A sanctions exemption for the humanitarian medical equipment was reportedly approved in May by the UN Security Council's North Korea sanctions committee.
- North Korea's willingness to accept the equipment has reportedly not been confirmed.

## How the original differs from reports

The original says "160 medical devices," but the reported scale is **1,431 items of 166 types**.

## Background

It was reported that three career service members were injured on Sept. 21 in a blast presumed to be from a mine during a DMZ search operation by the Army's 25th Division, and that who laid the mine was under investigation (Newspim). The "soldiers who were attacked" in the original appear to refer to this accident, but the poster did not say so explicitly.
