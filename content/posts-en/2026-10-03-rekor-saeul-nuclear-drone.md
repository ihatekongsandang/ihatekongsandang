This post is based on the original caption and first slide; it is a multi-slide post of which only the first slide was reviewed. The original cites an MBN exclusive (Oct. 2) and a Maeil Business Newspaper (매일경제) article; we checked the MBN report against the original.

## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- Citing an MBN exclusive, it says that in June an unidentified drone near the Saeul nuclear power plant in Ulju, Ulsan, came within 500 meters of the plant's fence, and although the top-level "serious" alert was issued, the jamming equipment did not work properly and the operator was not found.
- It says 101 unauthorized drones were detected around the five nuclear power plant headquarters from January to July, with no operator identified in 24 cases, and that only one of 35 national key facilities each had drone detection equipment and neutralization equipment.
- It concludes that "if a drone approached a nuclear plant and could be neither stopped nor caught, national key facilities are already defenseless."

## What reports confirm

- MBN reported on Oct. 2 that in June an unidentified drone was spotted about 2.75 km from the Saeul nuclear power plant in Ulju, Ulsan; it came within 500 meters of the plant's fence and the top-level "serious" alert was issued, but the jammer did not work properly and the operator was not caught.
- The same report said that in a drone-defense drill held a month before the incident, everything from detection to neutralization and catching the operator was completed in 10 minutes.
- It reported that 101 unauthorized drones were detected around the five nuclear power plant headquarters from January to July this year, with no operator identified in 24 cases, and that among 35 national key facilities managed by the Ministry of Science and ICT, only one each had drone detection equipment and drone neutralization equipment.
- People Power Party Rep. Park Jung-ha (박정하) reportedly said "(the drone drill) was nothing more than a show" and called for minimum protection standards for key national facilities and an effective counter-drone system.

## What we left out of the summary

- The drone's operator and purpose were not confirmed in the report.
- The "35 national key facilities" figure was reported for facilities under the Ministry of Science and ICT, not for all national key facilities.
- The conclusion that "national key facilities are already defenseless" is the poster's assessment.
- We could not locate the Maeil Business Newspaper article also cited by the original, so it was not checked.
