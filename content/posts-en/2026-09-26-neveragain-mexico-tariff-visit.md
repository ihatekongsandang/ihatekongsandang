## What the original post claims (the poster's opinion)

- It criticizes the president for going on an overseas trip during the holiday, claiming he leaves the country when things at home are in turmoil.
- It points out that Mexico has been imposing tariffs of up to 50% on some items from countries without an FTA, including South Korea, since January 2026, but that the summit only urged the resumption of FTA negotiations and did not secure the removal or reduction of the tariffs. It criticizes that while Korean companies are complaining of hardship, only photos of him eating ramyeon were highlighted.
- It writes that the president's domestic approval rating has plunged, which is fatal for diplomacy.

## What reports confirm

- Since Jan. 1, 2026, Mexico has been imposing tariffs of 5–35% on 1,463 items in "strategic industries" such as autos, steel and textiles from countries without an FTA, including South Korea, China and Vietnam, and 50% on some steel products. South Korea and Mexico have discussed an FTA since 2006 but have not concluded one.
- At a Sept. 24 summit with President Sheinbaum, President Lee reportedly said the resumption of Korea–Mexico FTA negotiations was necessary and proposed cooperation in electric vehicles and FA-50 cooperation for the Mexican Air Force's modernization project. No agreement to remove or reduce tariffs was reported.
- President Lee and the first lady visited the K-Expo in Mexico City and the Korean street there, where they tried Han River ramyeon, among other events on their schedule.

## What we left out of the summary

- We did not verify the "plunging approval rating," as the original does not cite any poll as its basis. "Fleeing abroad with no intention of taking responsibility" is the poster's assessment.
