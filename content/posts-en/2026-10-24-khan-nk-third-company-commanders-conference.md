This post is based on news reports. North Korea's announcements are as relayed by South Korean media from the Korean Central News Agency (KCNA) and the Rodong Sinmun.

## What reports confirm

- KCNA reported on Oct. 24 that leader Kim Jong Un (김정은) guided the Korean People's Army's 3rd Conference of Company Commanders held in Pyongyang on Oct. 22-23, according to reports. In a speech on "building a powerful army for a new era and the duties of company commanders," Kim reportedly called for "building the strongest army by fostering the strongest companies" and for continually improving company management and soldiers' lives "amid iron discipline."
- Top military officials, including Pak Jong-chon (박정천), vice chairman of the Workers' Party Central Military Commission, Defense Minister Kim Song-gi (김성기) and Chief of the General Staff Ri Yong-gil (리영길), reportedly attended. Kyunghyang Shinmun reported that this is the third time North Korean media have reported such a conference, after November 1991 and February 1999.
- Citing the Rodong Sinmun, Dong-A Ilbo reported that the conference discussed tasks and measures to raise companies' combat readiness and develop every company into an elite unit capable of carrying out combat missions on its own.
- KCNA reported on Oct. 28 that Kim had taken commemorative photos with participants the previous day, and Dong-A Ilbo reported that Kim called on company commanders to conduct intense training to complete combat readiness.
- The conference reports came after rumors of Kim's death spread on overseas social media. The National Intelligence Service reportedly told the National Assembly Intelligence Committee in a closed briefing on Oct. 23 that the death rumor was false (Kyunghyang Shinmun).

## What we left out of the summary

- The specifics of the death rumor (unsupported claims about illness and treatment) are not reproduced. Media interpretation of the conference's military meaning ("a move to tighten internal discipline and raise combat power") is the articles' analysis and was left out of the factual summary.
