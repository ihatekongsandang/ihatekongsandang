This post is based on the original caption ("[Report to the Public on the Lee Jae-myung Administration's Failures]") and the video thumbnail; the full video was not reviewed. The original is a post from Rep. Kim Tae-gyu (김태규)'s own account.

## What reports confirm

- On Sept. 22, 2026, the People Power Party reportedly held a "Report to the Public on the Lee Jae-myung Administration's Failures" on the steps of the National Assembly main building in Yeouido, Seoul, attended by party leader Jang Dong-hyuk (장동혁), floor leader Jung Jeom-sik (정점식) and others.
- Under the banners of "collapse of the livelihood economy," "destruction of diplomacy and security" and "seizure of the judiciary," the People Power Party reportedly criticized real estate policy, the tax overhaul, the nationwide farmland survey, the merger of the military academies, the abolition of prosecutors' supplementary investigation authority and the demand for a new Supreme Court justice recommendation, among other things.
- Leader Jang reportedly claimed, "The Lee Jae-myung government is now tearing down the house called South Korea from its foundations," and, on security, "It is trying to recognize North Korea as a state and to dismantle national security," taking issue with the response to the mine blast and the appointment of the defense minister, among other things.
- On the judiciary, he reportedly criticized the attempt to impeach the chief justice and the request for a new justice recommendation, saying, "They have dismantled the prosecution and seized control of the judiciary."

## What we left out of the summary

Statements such as "trying to recognize North Korea as a state" and "seized control of the judiciary" are political claims by the opposition leader, and the government's and ruling party's rebuttals were not included in the reports we found this time.
